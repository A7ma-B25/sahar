## Review Focus

1. A model that thinks (Gemma 4 template) or a server that ignores the "no thinking" switch: her turn must still end
   with a visible line and a logged drop reason, never «ما فهمت عليك» for every line.
2. A llama-server keep-alive connection closed between two turns (cpp-httplib 5 s): the second turn must not say
   load-failed — one retry on a fresh connection.
3. Her body below the work area (perched on the taskbar, flying, a small second monitor, 150 % DPI): every tag
   placement is finite and inside the monitor; no exception reaches the 1 s tick.
4. Another topmost window (the taskbar after a click, an always-on-top app) over her: she comes back on top within a
   second without flickering her own card/bubbles under her; in real fullscreen she stays hidden.
5. The settings app left open on «النماذج» for hours, minimised, or switched to another page: no more than one status
   request per 1.5 s while visible, none while hidden, none logged.
