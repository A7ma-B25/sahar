# Sahar 0.13: live activities (design spec)

Date: 2026-11-13 · Status: waiting for the owner's OK on the mockups · Canvas page: «الإصدار 13 — نشاطات حيّة»
Pictures: `sahar/docs/design/activities/` (`overview.png` first).

Now Bar (nixesty/now-bar, now "Pebbly") is only a source of ideas here. No code or assets are copied (GPL). Sahar is a
character, not a taskbar pill, so the activities live as things she carries, says and shows.

## 1. How activities live with her

Three directions were drawn (board `ActDirections`):

| | How | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. A tag she carries** (recommended) | A small porcelain tag hangs on a thread under her and holds one activity in one line. The others wait as beads on the thread. A click opens the card beside her. Moments go in her bubble. | A song, a percentage or a time left reads at a glance. It is hers: it sways with her and sleeps with her. One at a time, so no clutter. | Something is always under her while things run. Near the taskbar it must hang at her side. |
| 2. Inside the menu card | Nothing on screen but her face. A «هلق» section at the top of the menu card. Urgent things use the bubble. | Calmest. | You must open her to see anything. Progress and time left are hidden. |
| 3. She is the activity | No UI at all: props, expressions, and the bubble on hover. | Most soul. | The bubble goes after 8 s. Nothing reads at a glance. Hover is needed. |

**Recommendation:** direction 1, plus the best of 3. Her expressions stay (headphones and the sway, the look when
Claude asks), and the bubble is kept for moments only. The «هلق» section from 2 is not built: the menu header
already shows the most urgent fact (`MenuHeaderFact`), so activities only feed that header.

### 1.1 The tag
- Opaque porcelain (`menu` token), radius 14, height 40, max width 220, Rubik 13/500, one line with ellipsis. It has a
  5 px eyelet where the 1.5 px thread (`boundary` token) ties on. It holds an icon or a 24 px cover, the text, an
  optional meta (tabular digits) and an optional 2 px progress line under the text.
- Colour stays on her. The tag is neutral, except the asking state, which uses `accent-soft` / `accent-ink`. Alert icons
  use `warn`, and the camera/mic dot uses `moon`.
- **Placement:** under her when the work area has room (thread + 40 px); otherwise at the side with more room (the
  thread leaves from her side). Never above her, because the bubble lives there.
- **Motion:** it follows her on a critically damped spring (about 0.25 s). The sway is at most 4° and the position is
  snapped to whole pixels, so text never shimmers. It enters with opacity plus 4 px drop (0.22 s ease-out) and leaves
  in 0.15 s. With reduced motion there is no sway and no entrance motion.
- **Overlay:** the tag is a *need* rect for `OverlayPlan.Plan` (as the menu is). The window grows to hold it and
  `Uncover` still guarantees it never contains a monitor.
- The tag hides while the menu card or an activity card is open, while she is hidden, and while she is dragged.

### 1.2 The card
The same frame as the 0.11 menu card: 296 wide, padding 8, radius 22, a tinted header block of radius 14 (concentric
22 = 14 + 8), and the menu's shadow. It opens where the menu opens (`MenuPlace`) and closes as the menu does (Esc, click
outside, mouse away for 1.5 s). The asking card uses the `Alert` mood header tint.

### 1.3 Priority and stacking (board `ActRules`)
Only one tag shows at a time. Rank, high to low (equal rank: newest first):
1. Claude asks for permission. The card opens by itself (no focus steal, `WS_EX_NOACTIVATE`).
2. Moments (6 s): weather alert, new screenshot, copy done, Claude done, back from away, battery low, charging. The
   volume moment lasts 1.5 s.
3. Things you started and wait for: file copy, her timer.
4. Claude working.
5. Music playing.
6. Camera or microphone in use.
7. The shelf has items.
8. Music paused (10 min by default).
9. Weather today, only when nothing else is live.

The rest show as beads on the thread: at most 3, then a small knot. The mouse wheel over the tag cycles through them,
and a click on a bead brings that one forward. A moment takes the front for its 6 s and then gives the user's pick
back. When she is hushed (بسكت), tags still show but no bubble lines are said; the permission card still opens.

### 1.4 Fullscreen
The existing rule stays (`FullscreenScope`): if the fullscreen app is on her monitor she hides; on another monitor she
stays. While she hides:

| Activity | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Claude permission | A *knock*: a small tag drops from the top edge of that monitor for 4 s and goes back up. It has no buttons (a click in a game is dangerous); you answer in Claude or when you are back. |
| Storm, gale, heat alerts | Knock. Rain, frost and air quality wait. |
| Battery low | Knock. |
| Music, Claude working, copy, shelf, volume, screen time | Nothing. Finishes (Claude done, copy done) are kept for «وإنت غايب» *(owner decision)*. |
| Fullscreen on another monitor | Everything as usual, but no sounds. |

The knock window is its own small top-edge overlay, never focusable, and never covers the monitor (`Uncover`).

### 1.5 Expressions
- **Music:** headphones on (the approved accessory). Her halo takes the cover's colour, lightened so it stays soft. She
  sways gently at a calm fixed 0.5 Hz (GSMTC gives no tempo); the sway stops when the music stops.
- **Claude working:** focus mood. **Claude asking:** the existing attention face, and her eyes turn to you.
- **Shelf drop:** she catches the file with a small squish.
- **Rain coming:** her leaf droops a little, like an umbrella.
- **Back from away:** a happy hop on unlock.
- **Battery low:** sleepy.

The design board only adds optional `accessory`, `halo` and `still` props to `SaharOriginal`. The defaults are
unchanged.

## 2. Each activity

### Music (approved)
- **Source:** `Windows.Media.Control.GlobalSystemMediaTransportControlsSessionManager`. `Media.cs` polls it every 2 s
  today. Move it to events (`SessionsChanged`, per session `MediaPropertiesChanged`, `PlaybackInfoChanged`,
  `TimelinePropertiesChanged`) on a worker thread, with WinRT calls off the UI thread.
- **Which session:** the most recently playing one. Browser and video sessions follow `MediaEdges`: a video playing
  fullscreen never shows a tag.
- **Data:** `TryGetMediaPropertiesAsync()` gives the title, the artist and the `Thumbnail`. Decode the thumbnail at
  64×64 with `BitmapDecoder` and take a vivid median colour for her halo.
- **Controls:** `TryTogglePlayPauseAsync`, `TrySkipPreviousAsync`, `TrySkipNextAsync`.
- **Progress:** `GetTimelineProperties()`. Extrapolate `Position` from `LastUpdatedTime` while playing; the bar fills
  right to left in RTL. The transport row stays LTR.
- **Paused music:** stays 10 min (setting: 10 min / 1 h / always), then the tag goes.
- **Lyrics (opt-in):** off by default. The first tap on «الكلمات» asks inline: «الكلمات بتجي من الإنترنت (lrclib.net).
  بيروح بس اسم الأغنية والفنان.» [شغّلها] [لا].
  - Request: `GET https://lrclib.net/api/get?artist_name&track_name&album_name&duration`, then `/api/search`. The
    User-Agent is generic.
  - Synced LRC is cached in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sahar\lyrics\` under a hash key. A "none" answer is cached too.
  - There is only one host (no NetEase/Kugou fallbacks). The card shows two lines: previous and current.

### Weather (approved)
- **Source:** the Open-Meteo client already in `Senses/Weather.cs`, extended to
  `current=temperature_2m,weather_code&hourly=temperature_2m,weather_code,precipitation_probability,wind_gusts_10m&daily=temperature_2m_max,temperature_2m_min&forecast_hours=12`.
  Air quality comes from `air-quality-api.open-meteo.com` (`hourly=european_aqi`). This is a second host: list it on
  the Privacy page.
- **Rate:** once an hour (`WeatherRules.ShouldFetch`), plus once on wake and on unlock if the data is stale.
- **City logic (keeps prayer separate):**
  - `WeatherCity` (typed for the weather) wins.
  - If it is empty, the weather uses the prayer city's coordinates, and the page says «نفس مدينة الصلاة: …». If the
    prayer place is «موقع الويندوز», it is shown by that name.
  - Coordinates go out rounded to 0.01° (about 1 km).
  - The weather flow never writes `PrayerCity*`, and the prayer flow never writes `Weather*` (`SettingsBridge.City`
    `for` stays the switch).
  - Nothing is fetched unless `WeatherEnabled` (Privacy → الإنترنت → الطقس) is on.
- **Alerts:** each fires once per spell and resets after 1 h of calm. Thresholds:
  - rain: the existing `RainSoon`, within 2 h;
  - storm: codes 95–99 within 2 h;
  - wind: gusts ≥ 60 km/h within 3 h;
  - heat: today's max ≥ 38 °C (Gulf-friendly; a setting later);
  - frost: ≤ −1 °C within 12 h;
  - air: AQI ≥ 80.

  An alert is a moment: tag plus bubble («رح تشتي بعد ساعة تقريباً. خود شمسية!»).
- **Resting:** «٢٤° صحو», only when nothing else is live. The card shows the next 5 hours, today's min/max, any alert,
  and "Open-Meteo · قبل ١٠ د".

### Claude Code (approved)
- **Bridge:** `Sahar.Notify.exe` (Native AOT, already planned in the v1 spec), registered as Claude Code hooks in
  `~/.claude/settings.json`.
  - Async hooks: `UserPromptSubmit`, `PreToolUse`, `PostToolUse`, `PostToolUseFailure`, `Notification`, `Stop`,
    `SessionEnd`.
  - Synchronous hook: `PermissionRequest`, with a timeout of the wait setting plus 20 s.
  - It reads the hook JSON from stdin and sends one JSON line over the existing `sahar` named pipe (current-user ACL).
- **Data:** the step comes from the tool name and its target:
  - Edit/Write: «عم يعدّل» + the file name;
  - Read: «عم يقرا»;
  - Bash: «عم يشغّل أمر»;
  - Grep/Glob: «عم يدوّر»;
  - Web: «عم يدوّر عالنت».

  The task list comes from `TodoWrite` input. The project name is the `cwd` basename. Stop gives «Claude خلّص · N د».
- **Permission:** Allow/Deny from Sahar only for things she can show in full: a single-line Bash command, a file
  path, a URL or a search query. Multi-line commands, questions and plan approval show «Claude ناطر جوابك» with
  «افتح Claude». Both windows stay open; the first answer counts.
  - Answer: stdout `{"hookSpecificOutput":{"hookEventName":"PermissionRequest","decision":{"behavior":"allow"}}}`, or
    `"deny"` with a short message.
  - With no engine, no answer or a timeout, the hook prints nothing and exits 0 (Claude's own prompt takes over).
    *Check the exact schema against the current Claude Code hooks docs when building.*
- **Click safety:** buttons ignore clicks for the first 400 ms after the card appears, so a click aimed elsewhere can
  never answer.
- **Install:** «اربطها» in Settings → النشاطات.
  - It backs up `settings.json.before-sahar` once and `settings.json.sahar-last` on every write.
  - It touches only entries whose args start with `hook` from our exe, and repoints them if Sahar moves.
  - Removal puts the file back as it was, minus ours.

### Shelf (approved)
- **Drops:** OLE `RegisterDragDrop` on her overlay (her body is the hit target).
  - `CF_HDROP` keeps paths (no copies).
  - Images dragged from browsers (`CF_DIBV5`/PNG) are saved to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sahar\Shelf\`.
  - She shows the catch squish.
- **Drag out:** `DoDragDrop` with `DROPEFFECT_COPY` only, so a move can never delete the original.
- **Screenshots:** a `FileSystemWatcher` on `FOLDERID_Screenshots`. A new file waits until its size is stable, shows as
  a moment, then lands on the shelf.
- **Card:** 4 thumbnails (`IShellItemImageFactory`), a click selects, and the actions apply to the selection (or to all):
  - **Share:** `IDataTransferManagerInterop.ShowShareUIForWindow`.
  - **Copy text:** `Windows.Media.Ocr` (user profile languages; if Arabic OCR is missing she says so).
  - **Shrink:** JPEG with the long side at 1920, next to the original.
  - **Zip:** `ZipFile`, next to the originals.
  - **Empty the shelf:** «فضّي الرف».
- **Stored:** paths in `shelf.json`. Missing files drop out by themselves.

### Extras (need the owner's OK)
- **System:**
  - Volume: Core Audio `IAudioEndpointVolume` with `RegisterControlChangeNotify` (events). The moment lasts 1.5 s
    after a change, and never on start-up.
  - Battery: `Power.cs` plus `RegisterPowerSettingNotification` (`GUID_ACDC_POWER_SOURCE`,
    `GUID_BATTERY_PERCENTAGE_REMAINING`). The time to full comes from
    `Windows.Devices.Power.Battery.AggregateBattery.GetReport()`.
  - Mic/camera: `CameraMic.cs` (ConsentStore). Move it from a 5 s poll to `RegNotifyChangeKeyValue`, and read the app
    name only to show it.
- **While you were away:** `WTSRegisterSessionNotification` gives `WM_WTSSESSION_CHANGE` lock/unlock.
  - While locked, keep kinds, counts and times only.
  - On unlock, if the lock lasted ≥ 2 min and something happened: bubble «أهلين! صار ٤ شغلات وإنت غايب.», a tag
    moment, and the card (up to 5 rows, then «و٣ كمان»). It goes away after a minute.
- **File copy:**
  - Explorer's own dialog (`OperationStatusWindow`), as Now Bar reads it, read-only.
  - A `SetWinEventHook` on window show wakes the reader; while the dialog exists it is read once a second on a worker
    thread (title percentage, else UI Automation text).
  - The time left comes from our own smoothed pace.
  - «فرجيني نافذة النسخ» brings the dialog forward (after a user click).
  - `IFileOperation` is for doing copies, not watching Explorer's, so it is not used.
- **Screen time:**
  - Extend `AppTime`/`AppWatch`: seconds per app per day while not idle or locked (a playing video counts).
  - Stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sahar\usage\YYYY-MM-DD.json`, kept 7 days, and erased by `DataEraser`.
  - Shown in the card only (no tag of its own).
- Skipped: football, Galaxy Buds, game downloads.

## 3. Privacy
- **Logs** (`engine.log`, `Log.Write`) never hold song titles, artists, file names or paths, commands, URLs, typed
  text, window titles or city names. They hold only kinds and counts, for example `music: session playing`,
  `shelf: 3 items`, `claude: permission answered (allow)`. A redaction test enforces this (§5).
- **Lyrics** go out only after the inline consent and with Settings → الكلمات on. **Weather** goes out only with
  Privacy → الطقس on. Both send the minimum listed above. Screen time, the shelf and away notes stay on the PC.
- **During a call** (camera or mic in use), tags show the kind only («Claude ناطر جوابك», «الموسيقى»): no titles,
  commands or file names, because the screen may be shared.
- The Privacy page lists every host: open-meteo (forecast, geocoding, air quality) and lrclib.net.

## 4. Settings (new page «النشاطات», board `PSettingsActivities`)
- **شو تحمل:** the activity toggles, all on by default.
  - `ActMusic` (الموسيقى)
  - `ActWeather` (الطقس)
  - `ActClaude` (Claude Code)
  - `ActShelf` (الرف)
- **الجهاز:** the extras, shown only if approved.
  - `ActSystem` (الصوت والبطارية), on
  - `ActMicCam` (المايك والكاميرا), on
  - `ActCopy` (النسخ), on
  - `ActAway` (وإنت غايب), on
  - `ActScreenTime` (وقت الشاشة), off by default
- **الموسيقى:**
  - `LyricsEnabled` (off) and `LyricsConsented`
  - `MusicPausedKeep` (10 min / 1 h / always)
- **الطقس:** `WeatherCity` row («نفس مدينة الصلاة» when empty) with «غيّرها». A note points to Privacy for the internet
  switch.
- **Claude Code:**
  - link status with «اربطها/افصلها» (derived from the file, not stored)
  - `ClaudeAnswerFromSahar` (on)
  - `ClaudeWaitSeconds` (30 / 60 / 300; default 60)
- **الرف:** `ShelfScreenshots` (on).
- Each key gets a `SettingsPatch` validator, and `SettingsBridge.Pages` gains `activities`. Older settings files keep
  the defaults: setters, not `init`, as `SaharSettings` already notes.

## 5. Test ideas
- **Stack (Core, pure):**
  - rank order;
  - equal rank → newest;
  - moments expire at 6 s / 1.5 s and give the pick back;
  - beads capped at 3 plus a knot;
  - wheel cycling;
  - the tag hidden while a card or the menu is open.
- **Placement:**
  - below when room, else the side with more room;
  - never above;
  - with the tag need, `OverlayPlan.Plan` never `Covers` a monitor (1 and 2 monitors, all four taskbar edges,
    100 % and 150 % DPI);
  - whole-pixel positions.
- **Fullscreen matrix:**
  - knock kinds only;
  - nothing on another monitor except muted sounds;
  - the knock rect is never a whole monitor.
- **Weather:** fixtures for each alert threshold edge, once per spell, 1 h calm reset, and the city resolution (weather
  city › prayer city › none; Windows location label; 0.01° rounding).
- **Claude hooks:**
  - parse fixtures of every event (→ step text, todos, project);
  - eligibility (single-line vs multi-line, questions, plan);
  - decision JSON;
  - timeout or no engine → empty stdout and exit 0;
  - install/remove/follow on `settings.json` fixtures (other hooks untouched, backups written once);
  - a click within 400 ms is ignored.
- **Lyrics:** LRC parser, the line at time t, a cached "none", and no request without consent (a fake HTTP handler
  counts the calls).
- **Media:** session choice, paused keep, position extrapolation, cover colour from fixture images (lightness clamp
  for the halo).
- **Copy:** pace smoothing and time-left estimate (fast copy, stall, finish moment).
- **Away:** a lock under 2 min → nothing; counts and kinds only; row cap.
- **Screen time:** idle excluded, video counted, day rollover, 7-day pruning, erased by `DataEraser`.
- **Log redaction:** run each scenario with sentinel strings (a fake title, path, command, city) and assert none of
  them reach `engine.log`.
- **Visual (manual):**
  - light/dark, reduced motion, RTL with English titles inside;
  - Arabic diacritics: «سَحَر» only at ≥ 24 px, plain «سحر» in small labels;
  - 100 %/150 % DPI;
  - a tag over a white app and over a dark one;
  - no flicker when she flies with a tag.

## Owner rulings (2026-11-13) — binding, override anything above

2. Extras: **all four** — system + mic/camera, while you were away, file copying, screen time. (Football, Galaxy Buds, game downloads: not built.)
