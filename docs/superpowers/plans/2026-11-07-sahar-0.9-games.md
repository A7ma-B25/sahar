# Sahar 0.9 — Games Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** "العبي" opens four games with Sahar — الحقني, غمّضلي, الطابة, لقط النجوم — plus a random move.

**Architecture:** Pure rules in `Sahar.Core.Games` (flee target, hide spot, ball physics with gravity, falling stars, result lines). `Sahar.Engine.Games.GamePlayer` runs a game as motion 37 `Game` (its tick moves her each frame), an invisible full-screen `GameCatcher` takes the mouse, Esc / right-click / another window ends it, and she says the score. Life waits while a game runs; anything that takes her over (hiding, goodbye) ends the game quietly.

**Spec:** the user's 0.9 choices (2026-11-07) on top of docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-sahar-windows-v1-design.md §7.1 (menu).

## Global Constraints
- Her words go through `Address.ToYou`; no sounds yet; nothing logged but the game, its end and the score.

## Review Focus
1. A game on a second monitor or with a different DPI: the ball, stars and her stay on her monitor.
2. Leaving a game any way (Esc, right-click, another window, hiding, quitting) never leaves the catcher up (the mouse would be stuck).
3. Hide and seek never leaves her off-screen after the game.
4. A flick far off the screen, or no flick at all: the ball still lands and she still fetches it.
5. Stars do not pile up as visuals (memory) during a full 40 s game.
