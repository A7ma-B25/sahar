**Goal:** Sahar keeps her original rig exactly, gains a warm edge on her outer stars, a night sky with stars inside her lower body and a happy face, and moves with one Composition-driven "soul" layer (float, follow-through, sway, leaf lag, hop, look, blink, idle actions) instead of today's separate idle clocks.

## Rulings (ambiguities in the spec, decided here)

1. **Distances of §4 are rig units, not DIP.** Float 6 and hop peak 38 are taken in the 300-unit rig space, as in the approved `SaharOriginal` mockup (its transforms are SVG user units) and as the shadow formula needs (`rx = 62 − 0.5·height` is in rig units: a 38-unit peak gives rx 43, opacity 0.084; read as "DIP@Medium" = 76 units the shadow would vanish at 0.008). So at Medium the float is 3 DIP and the hop 19 DIP, and both scale with size. — Cost if wrong: two constants (`SoulMath.FloatUnits`, `HopPeakUnits`) ×2. **Owner to confirm** (listed in the 0.10c handoff).
2. **Follow-through sign follows the mockup:** `scaleY = 1 + 0.012·cos φ + 0.012·sin(2πt/3.6)` (she stretches while rising; the mockup's `0.012 * vy/6 * -1` with `vy = −6 cos φ`). The spec's "(−cos φ)" names the velocity term. — Cost: one sign.
3. **Star edge width scales with each star** (1.3 in the star's own units, as in the mockup where the stroke sits inside `scale(s)`). — Cost: one parameter.
4. **Wash colours:** start Calm from L2's exact stops (`#8E7BE0` / `#A897EA` / `#CBC1F0`), other moods from the §3 formula (inner = `glow` −0.12 OKLab L, mid = `glow`, outer = `bottom` @0); then for every mood darken inner and mid together in steps of 0.02 OKLab L (max 15) until white measures ≥ 1.8:1 at the wash centre (120,196). Calm needs one step (≈`#8875D9`/`#A291E3`); Happy, Excited, Online, Cold need 7–10 steps. The table is computed in `design.py` (the art's one source) and exported as `sky.washStops`; Core re-checks the contrast. — Why: the spec's check fails for 9 moods without it. Cost: a table of 16 colour triples.
5. **Which clocks the soul replaces:** bob, shadow breathing, blink and leaf sway become the soul; the moon lap becomes 16 s; hem flutter, halo and bud-glow breathing, star-ring spin and twinkles stay as they are (original-look features). — Cost: small.
6. **Blink randomness without CPU:** 8 gaps drawn once at start from [2.6, 6] s (seeded) form one keyframe loop (≈ 35 s). — Why: "random 2.6–6 s" and "Composition-driven" both hold. Cost: none visible.
7. **Look easing** "12 %/frame equiv." (time constant ≈ 0.13 s) is a 400 ms keyframe from the current value with `cubic-bezier(0.2,0.9,0.3,1)`, restarted on each pointer update (≤ 30 Hz). — Cost: feel only.
8. **A tap still plays the `poked` expression** (state `poked`, as the `motion` stage checks) **and** the soul hop with the Joy face; while a hop runs, `ExpressionPlayer` does not start the `Squish` extra (the hop is the squish). `celebrate`'s squish is unchanged.

## Review Focus

1. **A size or DPI change in mid-hop or mid-float** (dragging her onto a 150 % monitor, `debug-display-changed`): every soul animation restarts with the new pixel values; she never ends offset or squashed. → Task 5 UI step "the soul survives a rescale".
2. **A hop asked while she is not resting** (dragged, thrown, sitting, grounded expression, sulking, a game, hidden, reduced motion): no hop starts; the tap still pokes. → Task 4 `SoulRules_CanHop_*` tests; Task 5 UI step "no hop while dragged".
3. **Reduced motion switched while she moves:** loops stop at rest values, a running hop is cut to rest, the moon rests beside her; switched back, everything resumes. → Task 6 UI step with `debug-reduced-motion`.
4. **The pointer on another monitor, far away, or exactly on her eyes:** look is zero beyond 400 DIP and at zero distance (no NaN). → Task 4 `Look_*` tests.
5. **An event face arriving during the 1.1 s Joy face** (timer done, Claude permission): when the Joy face ends she shows the event's face, not a stale one. → Task 6 UI step "the joy face gives way to the newest expression".
