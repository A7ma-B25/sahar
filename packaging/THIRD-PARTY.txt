The models are not in this zip: you download the one you choose from Hugging Face inside Sahar, each checked by its
SHA-256. The recommended ones are Apache-2.0: Gemma 4 E4B / E2B (Google; GGUF by unsloth) and Jais 3 (Inception/MBZUAI;
GGUF by IFM). The optional CUDA pack (llama.cpp b12207 CUDA 12.4 build + NVIDIA's cudart DLLs, from the same
official release) is downloaded only when you choose it.
