# Sahar 0.17 — the online brain (for words only)

*2026-11-27. Phase 1 of the owner-approved Jarvis roadmap (`/home/claude/sahar-design/specs/2026-11-27-jarvis-roadmap.md`).
Scope approved by the owner; rulings taken while building are in the ledger (`.superpowers/sdd/0.17-ledger.md`) and
copied to §12 at the end.*

## 1. Goal and the one felt success criterion

He opens the chat window, types a normal question or «اكتبلي…», and Sahar answers **in her own voice, fast and as long
as it needs**, from an online model he chose — while everything that *does* something (timers, music, prayer, games,
notes, her moves, her own initiative) stays with her local brain exactly as in 0.16.

Felt criterion (research lesson 15): *"In the chat window she starts answering in under 2 s and it sounds like Sahar."*

Principle: **online brain for WORDS only; the local brain keeps ALL actions.** No tool ever runs from an online reply.

## 2. What the research tells us (lessons applied)

- **L1 latency:** her body reacts before any token (thinking face at once); words stream; short context (~4 k tokens);
  time-to-first-token is measured per brain and logged as numbers.
- **L2 route before you think:** deterministic router (no LLM routing); fast path first; quota visible; background turns
  never spend online calls; when the quota ends she says so once, in character.
- **L3 secrets:** the key is typed by him, sealed with DPAPI (CurrentUser) by the Mind into her SecretBox; never in
  settings.json, never logged, never in the repo or CI.
- **L5 small models and tools:** tools stay with the local decision-JSON loop (untouched); the online request has no
  tools at all.
- **L8 one persona:** both brains get the same persona and the same fixed safety rules (one `PromptParts.Persona`); a
  tiny badge says which brain answered, so a weaker local answer reads as "Sahar offline", not "Sahar got dumber".

## 4. Architecture

```
chat window line ─► engine fast path ─(free text)─► turn {source:chat, window:true, brain:<toggle>}
                                                         │
                                          Sahar.Mind ─► BrainRouter.Decide (pure, Core)
                                             ├── Local  ─► TurnRunner (decision JSON, tools; unchanged)
                                             └── Online ─► OnlineTurn: ONE streamed plain-text chat-completions
                                                           request per provider in the chain (no tools, no JSON);
                                                           all online failed ─► TurnRunner (local), told once
```

- **`ProviderProfile`** (Core, `Mind/Online`): id, base URL, model, auth header name/scheme, `LlamaFields`, extra headers,
  extra body, `UseProxy`, quota kind, default daily limit. `ProviderProfiles.Local(port, key)`, `Groq(model)`,
  `OpenRouter(model)`, `Custom(url, model)`.
- **`OpenAiClient`** is generalised (no second client): `OpenAiClient(handler, profile, apiKey)`; the old
  `(handler, port, key)` constructor builds the local profile. Status and transport errors map to its own codes (§7).
  Rate headers are read into `LlmReply.Rate`. It still writes JSON with `Utf8JsonWriter` and parses with `JsonDocument`
  (AOT), and never logs text.
- **`BrainRouter`** (Core, pure): see §5.
- **`OnlineTurn`** (Core): builds the online prompt (§6), walks the chain, retries once on 429/5xx with backoff,
  streams `partial` (cumulative words) to the engine, sends one `say` with `Brain = "online"`, keeps the history, counts
  the quota, and returns "fell through" so the host runs the local turn.
- **`OnlineState`** (Core): per provider per day: requests used (local count, failed attempts too), the provider's own
  remaining/limit when known, exhausted-today, and whether he was already told today. Kept in her SecretBox
  (`online-state`), reset by day.
- **`OnlineKeys`** (Core): the sealed keys (`online-keys` in her SecretBox: AES-GCM under the DPAPI-wrapped key). Only
  the Mind writes or reads it. The settings app never stores the key; it passes it once over the engine pipe to the Mind.
- **`TurnHistory`** (Core): the last turns, persisted encrypted (`turn-history` in her SecretBox), at most 40 pairs,
  pruned by `MindEpisodeDays`, wiped by «انسي كل شي» (crypto-shred of the box key). Local reads its 6 turns / 800 tokens
  from it as before (no local slowdown); online reads up to the ~4 k-token prompt cap.
- **Protocol:** `MindMsg` gains `Brain` (turn: `auto|local|online`; say/partial: `local|online`), `Window` (turn from the
  chat window) and a new pair `online` (engine → mind: `op = key|forget|test|status|bench`) / `online-status` (mind →
  engine: providers, keys present, quota, last test result, bench). Unknown fields are ignored by old readers.
- **Settings** (settings.json, nothing secret): `MindLocalOnly`, `MindOnlineConsent`, `MindOnlineChain` (ordered provider
  ids; local is always last), `MindGroqModel`, `MindOpenRouterModel`, `MindCustomUrl`, `MindCustomModel`,
  `MindChatBrain` (`online|local`, the chat window's toggle), `MindOnlineKeys` (which providers have a sealed key — a
  flag list the engine writes from the Mind's status, so the chat toggle can hide without asking the Mind).
- **No restart:** the Mind reads settings each turn; the chain and models apply to the next line.

## 5. The router (deterministic, pure, tested)

`BrainRouter.Decide(RouteInput) → (Brain, Reason)`, in this order:

1. `source` is not `chat` (initiative, routine, reflect, draft, voice) → **local** (`background` / `draft`). Drafts use
   his style samples from imported chats: always local.
2. `MindLocalOnly` → **local** (`local-only`).
3. No consent → **local** (`no-consent`).
4. Not from the chat window → **local** (`card`). (Card lines that ask for writing/explaining are moved to the chat
   window by the engine first: §8.)
5. The toggle says `local` → **local** (`toggle`).
6. The line looks like an action (`ActionIntent`: timers, reminders, music, prayer, weather, Quran, notes, games,
   moves, hush, sleep, open/close, «شغّلي/وقّفي/حطّيلي/ذكّريني…», English equivalents) → **local** (`action`).
7. No provider in the chain with a key that is not exhausted today → **local** (`no-provider` / `exhausted`).
8. Otherwise → **online** (`chat`).

`WritingIntent` («اكتبلي/اكتبيلي/اشرحلي/اشرحيلي/فسّريلي/ترجميلي/لخّصيلي/حلّلي…», write/explain/translate/summarise,
or ≥ 160 characters) is used by the engine for the card (§8) and for the suggestion (§9), never to send online silently.

## 6. The online prompt (what leaves the laptop)

One request per turn: `stream:true`, `max_tokens:1500`, `temperature:0.7`, `top_p:0.9`, no tools, no `response_format`.

- **system:** `PromptParts.Persona(...)` (his persona file + the fixed safety rules — identical for both brains) +
  `OnlineRules` (plain text, Syrian, as long as needed in the chat window, simple markdown allowed: bold, lists, code;
  no images; never claims she did something; nothing to do with tools here — "if he wants a timer or music, it happens
  when he asks in his own words", no «أقل من ٢٢٠ حرف», no "I'm a small program" refusals for writing/explaining).
- **history:** from `TurnHistory`, newest first, until the prompt reaches ~4 000 estimated tokens; a pair is skipped
  when either line trips `Sensitive.Check` or the distress check.
- **user:** the moment (time of day and weekday only) + his confirmed profile/memories **filtered** + his line.

**Never sent online** (`OnlinePolicy`, pure, tested): sensitive-tagged profile items or memories (anything but
`Sensitivity.None`, or a text the Core keyword check tags), profile items learnt from imported chats or music, style
samples, foreground app names/titles, music titles, Claude Code activity, weather/city, battery, prayer, the action log.
A distress cue never reaches the online request: the fixed reviewed reply is sent locally first (same as today), and
`SayGuard` runs on every online `say` (the Mind's single `Send`).

**Consent (one time):** the «العقل أونلاين» page shows, before the key field works, what is sent («كلامك بالمحادثة،
وآخر حكينا، وشغلات أكّدتلي ياها مو حساسة») and what never is, and that free models may keep and train on what they
get. «موافق» sets `MindOnlineConsent`. Without it the router never goes online.

## 7. Errors, retries, quota

| Code | When | Chain | Key test line |
|---|---|---|---|
| `key-bad` | 401 | next provider | «المفتاح غلط» |
| `blocked` | 403 (region/policy) | next | «محجوب أو ما في نت» |
| `no-credit` | 402 | next | «الرصيد خلص عندهن» |
| `quota` | 429 (after the one retry), or remaining = 0 | next; provider marked exhausted today | «خلصت طلبات اليوم» |
| `server` | 5xx (after the one retry) | next | «المزوّد واقع هلق، جرّب بعدين» |
| `timeout` | no first byte in 12 s or no byte for 20 s | next | «محجوب أو ما في نت» |
| `offline` | DNS / connect / TLS failure | next | «محجوب أو ما في نت» |
| `stream-error` | an SSE `{"error":…}` or `finish_reason:"error"` mid-stream | next | — |
| `bad-output` | 2xx but no words | next | — |

- Retry once on 429 and 5xx: wait `Retry-After` (capped at 3 s) or 800 ms. Every attempt, failed or not, counts in the
  local quota count (OpenRouter counts failed attempts).
- Provider error bodies are never read into a log; logs carry the code, the HTTP status number and the provider id.
- OpenRouter: `/api/v1/key` read when the page asks and after each OpenRouter call (it costs no quota). Groq: the RPD
  headers of each reply. Others: our count. Settings shows «اليوم: ١٢ من ٥٠» (or «اليوم: ١٢» with no known limit).
- All online exhausted (quota): she tells him **once a day**, in character («خلّصت طلبات الأونلاين لليوم 🌙 كمّلت من
  عقلي المحلي.») as a short line before the local answer; then local silently for the rest of the day.

## 8. Engine and surfaces

- **Chat window:** a small brain chip in the header, «أونلاين» / «محلي» (tap toggles `MindChatBrain`), hidden when
  online is not set up (no consent, no key, or «محلي فقط») — hidden, not disabled. Each of her messages carries a tiny
  badge «أونلاين» or «محلي» (persisted in `ChatEntry.Brain`). Streamed words as in 0.16 (throttled partials).
- **Safe rendering:** her text is rendered by a tiny markdown subset (`md.js`: paragraphs, **bold**, *italic*, `code`,
  fenced code, `-`/`1.` lists, `#` headings as bold lines) into DOM nodes via `h()` (never `innerHTML`); links stay
  plain text (never clickable, never auto-opened); no images (CSP `img-src 'self' data:` and no `<img>` from text).
- **Card:** a card line with `WritingIntent` and online allowed opens the chat window and goes there as a window turn
  (the long answer lives in the chat; the card and her bubble stay short). Every other card line stays local as today.
- **Bubble:** unchanged rules (≤ 60 chars streamed; long → clamped + «كمّل بالمحادثة»); chat-window turns never stream
  into the bubble.
- **Thinking pose:** when a chat turn starts she shows her focus face («•••») at once, back to her own face on the
  answer or an error (only over her idle/music face, never over an event's face).
- **Latency:** the Mind logs `online: <provider> first=<ms> total=<ms> out=<tokens>` and the local turn line as today.

## 9. Settings page «العقل أونلاين» (Porcelain, after «عقلي»)

1. Status card: which brain answers in the chat now, and «محلي فقط» (switch). With it on, everything below is hidden
   and nothing leaves the laptop.
2. Consent card (until «موافق»).
3. «المزوّد» rows in chain order (Groq, OpenRouter, custom), each: name, model (select of suggestions + «غيره…» text),
   key field (password, never shown back: «في مفتاح محفوظ ✓» + «امسحه»), «احفظ», «جرّب المفتاح» → one plain Arabic
   line (§7 table, «يشتغل ✓» when good), quota «اليوم: ١٢ من ٥٠», up/down to reorder; custom also has its URL.
4. «جرّب عقلي» (SHOULD): ≤ 5 short requests to the first provider, with a warning line that it uses 5 of today's
   requests; shows first-word time and a sample answer; and a one-click «رجّعني عالمحلي» (sets `MindChatBrain = local`).
5. Note: free models may keep what they get; keys stay on this laptop, locked to his Windows user.

Suggestion (SHOULD): in the chat window with the toggle on «محلي», a line with `WritingIntent` gets a one-line inline
ask once per conversation: «هيدا بيطلع أحلى أونلاين، بدك؟» — «إي» sends this line online, «لا» keeps it local. Never a
silent switch.

## 10. Limits removed (online and the chat window only)

- Online: `max_tokens` 1 500; no 220-character rule; long answers allowed.
- The fixed rule «إنتِ برنامج صغير…» becomes «إنتِ برنامج على جهازو، مش إنسانة…» plus «إذا طلب تكتبي أو تشرحي، ساعديه
  بالطول اللي بيلزم» — for both brains (one persona). The local decision rules (220 chars, 256 tokens) stay for the card
  and her bubble: her local brain is small and the card is short.
- Safety rules are unchanged (distress reply, labels, no diagnosis, data blocks are data).

## 12. Rulings (taken while building; ledger `.superpowers/sdd/0.17-ledger.md`)

1. The page is «العقل أونلاين» (id `online`), placed after «شخصيتي» (0.16 pins «شخصيتي» right after «عقلي»): «العقل»
   beside «عقلي» would read as the same page.
2. Card lines never go online; a card line with WritingIntent (online usable, chip not «محلي») moves to the chat window
   as a window turn. Badges therefore live in the chat window only.
3. Consent is given once on the page (before any key field shows); no extra chat-time consent screen. The consent
   names «اسمك» too: the persona addresses him by name, and the persona goes to both brains.
4. Defaults: Groq `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, OpenRouter `google/gemma-4-31b-it:free`, chain groq → openrouter → custom →
   local. Their Syrian-dialect quality is unverified.
5. The thinking pose is her existing focus face («•••», the `claude-working` expression), only over her idle/music face.
6. The fixed rule loses only the word «صغير» and gains a writing/explaining help line, for both brains; the distress
   reply text is a safety text and is unchanged.
7. Keys and today's counts live in her SecretBox: «انسي كل شي» takes them too (he types the key again).
8. With online usable, the chat window answers even before a local model is installed (actions still need it).
9. `SayGuard` runs on every online `say` and (review I3) on every streamed online partial, and history keeps the
   guarded line. Its word list is a safety rule and stays identical for both brains (WON'T for 0.17), so a technical
   answer naming «تشخيص»/"diagnostics"/"disorder" still becomes the listening line — an owner decision for later.
10. Review C1: a 429 is a cooldown (Retry-After, else 45 s); only the provider's own daily numbers end its day (Groq's
    request headers; OpenRouter's key counter, on UTC days). Groq's prompt is capped at ~2.5 K tokens (8 K tokens/min).
11. Review I1: only earlier online pairs (chat-window answers written online) go online; «حكي جديد» and deleting a
    conversation clear them. Review I2: a distress cue routes local (the runner keeps its quiet day).
12. Review I5: when no provider could be reached, online rests 3 minutes and she says «ما في نت هلق، عم جاوبك من عندي»
    once; no first word within 45 s ends the online try.
13. Review I4: in the chat window digits stay as written in code, links and Latin words; prose numbers read Arabic.
