## 1. Why

The owner's complaints about 0.14.0:

1. White native Win32 surfaces that are not Porcelain.
2. A speech bubble off-identity: white in dark mode too, Segoe UI, emoji, one fixed 260×52 line.
3. No real chat place.
4. A hide-and-seek built on text prompts («غمّض عيونك… 🙈») with a dumb hide: a quarter of her at a random screen edge.
5. The tag needs to sit next to the new bubble.

- **Gold #F2C86A** is hers. It is used for the clasp, the live dot and the game's warm hints.
- **Type.** Rubik: 15 body, 17/600 heads, 13 meta, with tabular numerals.
- **Icons.** 24-grid strokes at 1.7 with the existing hand-wobble filter. No emoji anywhere in the UI or in her lines; the owner decides whether her lines lose their emoji too.

## 4. Surfaces

### 4.1 Speech bubble (`V15Bubble`)

- **Placement.** It sits above her head, centred on her, and follows her through the existing expression animation, clamped to the work area. The surface is the menu colour, radius 18, padding 11/15. A 22×11 rounded tail points at her leaf.
- **Size.** Max width 264. Text wraps with `text-wrap: pretty`.
- **Variants:**
  - **say.** Up to 3 lines. It stays for 8 s plus 60 ms per character, capped at 14 s. A click on her or on the bubble dismisses it.
  - **ask.** A question plus «إي» (primary) and «لا» (secondary). Keys: 1/2, Enter is «إي», Esc is «لا». No answer within 2 min counts as «لا» (AskDrill rule). The bubble stays until answered and does not time out at 8 s. One ask at a time; others queue.
  - **list.** 2–4 rows, each with an icon and a label (games, gender, update). It takes keyboard focus; arrows move, Enter picks, Esc closes. Five or more items open a picker panel instead.
  - **long.** Clamped to 3 lines plus «كمّل بالمحادثة», which opens the chat on that message.
- **Interaction.** The bubble takes focus only for ask and list. say is no-activate (`WS_EX_NOACTIVATE`) and never steals focus from his work.
- **Collisions.** A new say replaces the old one with a cross-fade. An open ask keeps priority; says queue behind it for 2 s and then show as a tag-height whisper.

### 4.2 Tag (`V15Tag`)

- **Look.** A 32 px pill under her, hung from a 12 px thread (boundary colour, 70%). The 0.13 beads are gone; one tag shows at a time.
- **Content.** Icon (t2), up to 2 words, and an optional tabular meta.
  - A live job adds a 6 px gold dot breathing at 2.4 s.
  - A job that needs him uses soft/softInk.
  - A warning colours only the icon (warn).
  - Progress is a 2 px hairline inside the pill.
- **Rule from 0.14.1.** Only live and important things show.
- **With the bubble.** The tag and the bubble never overlap: bubble above, tag below.

### 4.3 Picker panel (`V15Picker`)

- **Geometry.** The panel opens beside her like the menu card: same side rules, the gold clasp at her face height, 320 wide, radius 22, padding 8.
- **Header.** Tint, a 17/600 title, and one line of context. The context is a chip (reciter, city) or a hint.
- **Search field.** A sunken field with real text input (the ChatField technique). Matching is by name or by number. Arabic folding ignores tashkeel, hamza forms, ة/ه and ى/ي.
- **List.** Rows are 42–44 px with a hover tint; the selected row is soft. A thin themed scrollbar sits on the logical end side.
- **Fixed height.** The panel height never changes while typing, so the panel does not jump.
- **Empty and no-result states.** Each has one icon, a line, and what to try.
- **Footer.** «Esc لسكّر»; the Quran shows a now-playing strip with pause and stop.
- **Keyboard.** Type to filter, ↑/↓, Enter, Esc. Back (→ in RTL, or Backspace on an empty field) leaves a drill. Clicking elsewhere closes it (WM_ACTIVATE, as today).
- **Quran:**
  - «كمّل الكهف · وقفت عند ١٢:٠٤» is the first row when there is a last surah. The resume position is new; today only the surah is kept.
  - The reciter is a drill that returns to the surah list.
- **Clipboard.** «امسح الكل» asks «أكيد؟» inline: the footer swaps to «إي، امسح» / «لا».
- **Prayer.** The next prayer is soft with «بعد ٤٢ د»; past ones use t3. «طريقة الحساب» is a drill.

### 4.4 Input card (`V15Input`)

- **Geometry.** Same placement as the picker. 300 wide, with a tint header (her question) and one sunken field with a real input.
- **Controls.** One primary button. A secondary («تخطّى», «بعدين») appears only where skipping is allowed. «Esc لسكّر» is shown as a hint.
- **Errors.** Shown in warn under the field. The field gets a 1.5 px warn ring and the text is kept.
- **Kinds:**
  - **timer.** Minutes with «دقيقة» as a suffix; helper «من دقيقة لـ ١٠ ساعات».
  - **note.** Up to 3 lines of text plus the last 3 notes, dimmed.
  - **city.** Suggestions while typing, debounced 300 ms. Offline gives an error with «جرّب كمان مرة».
  - **name.** First run; she introduces herself and the field is optional.
- **Privacy.** Nothing typed is logged (as today).

### 4.5 Chat window (`V15Chat`)

- **Opening.** From the card's chat row by «كمّل بالمحادثة» or Ctrl+Enter, from a long bubble, and from the tray. It opens beside her on her monitor (400×620 DIP, radius 16, Win11 rounding) and remembers its position per monitor.
- **Header.**
  - Her face cropped in a 40 px tint circle; it uses her live expression when rendered natively.
  - «سحورة», plus a status line: the conversation title, or «عم تكتب…» in softInk.
  - Buttons for كل الحكي, حكي جديد and سكّر.
- **Thread.** RTL, styled like WhatsApp in Arabic:
  - His bubbles sit on the left (menu colour); hers sit on the right (tint, her colour).
  - Grouped bubbles cut the corner only on the first of a group.
  - Day chips read «مبارح» / «اليوم» / date.
  - Times sit under each group in t3 tabular; his carry a ✓ for "she got it".
  - The view starts at the latest message. New messages scroll only if he is already at the bottom; otherwise a «↓ جديد» chip appears.
  - Text is selectable and copyable with themed selection.
- **Typing state.** Her bubble with three dots at 1.2 s staggered, and the header status. Streamed words replace the dots as they arrive (the Mind's `partial`).
- **Action receipts.** A plain line inside her bubble, for example «مؤقت ساعة · خلص ٩:١٢ م».
- **Draft («بصوتك»).** Her message is followed by a draft block: menu colour, «بصوتك» with a pen icon, the text, and «انسخها». After copying the button reads «نسختها» with a check for 2 s. Underneath: «ما ببعتها لحدا، إنت بتلصقها». She never sends anything.
- **Inline ask («بتسمحلي؟»).** The question shows inside her bubble with «إي، …» and «لا». Once answered, the buttons collapse to a receipt line («سمحتلها · ٩:٣١», or «ما سمحتلها»). Timeout behaves as «لا»: «ما جاوبت، فما عملت شي». An ask raised while the window is open goes here, not to the bubble.
- **Composer.** The menu-colour field «احكي معي…», mic, and send. Send is disabled when the field is empty. Enter sends and Shift+Enter makes a new line. There is a 500-character limit, the same as ChatField.
- **Conversation list.**
  - «حكي جديد» at the top.
  - Conversations grouped by اليوم / هالأسبوع / أقدم, each with title, last line and time.
  - The title is set automatically from the first line, about 4 words.
  - Right-click or the context key offers «سمّيه» / «امسحه» («أكيد؟»).
  - Footer: «محفوظ عندك بس، ومقفول. بينمسح لحاله بعد ٣٠ يوم», using the live MindEpisodeDays value.
- **Empty state.** She looks at him; «لسا ما حكينا» plus 3 starters.
- **Notifications.** No conversation content ever goes to Windows notifications or toasts, and none goes to the tray tooltip. If she answers while the window is closed, she shows a say bubble only if he is not in full screen and not in DND. That bubble carries a content-free line («ردّيت عليك») plus her tag; the words stay in the window.
- **Light and dark.** Follow the settings app theme. Both are drawn.

### 4.6 Tray menu (`V15Tray`)

- **Contents.** A header (her face, plus «صاحية، وهادية»), then:
  - an activity segmented control (هادية / متوسطة / نشيطة);
  - إعداداتي, فرجيني حركاتك, خبّيني ساعة, الطقس (city meta), مين أنا؟ and افتح مجلدي;
  - a divider, then «سكّري سحورة».
- **Language and position.** One language, following the app. It opens above the tray icon on whichever side the taskbar's tray is (the left in RTL Windows).

### 4.7 Settings-start error

Shown when WebView2 is missing. A Porcelain card with her face, «ما قدرت افتح الإعدادات», one line of why, «نزّلها» (opens the Microsoft page in the browser) and «سكّر». It is drawn by the engine, which already has D2D, instead of MessageBoxW.

## 5. Hide-and-seek («الغميضة») rules and animation

Per round:

1. **Start, with no text.**
   - She covers her eyes with two small paws (the `cover` pose) and blushes; one paw sits low enough to show a cheeky sliver of eye.
   - Over her head three tint discs pop in sequence: «٣», «٢», «١». Each lasts 0.7 s, scales from 0.6 to 1 with an overshoot, then fades.
   - Three round pips sit under her.
   - Press Esc to stop at any time.
2. **Hide.** She shrinks with a squash and zips off with a trail of 3 tiny gold sparks (fading over 0.6 s). The trail is a decoy: it curves, it doesn't point.
3. **Hiding spots, picked smartly.** The engine has the window list, the taskbar and monitor geometry. The candidates are:
   - (a) **Behind the edge of a visible top-level window.** Her visual is clipped to the window's rect so she looks *behind* it, peeking with one eye (the `peek` pose). The spot must be inside the work area and not on a full-screen window.
   - (b) **Behind the taskbar.** Only her leaf and bud poke above it; the leaf sways at 1.6 s.
   - (c) **A screen edge, as today.** She shows a quarter and blinks.
   - Weights are (a) 50%, (b) 30%, (c) 20%. A spot is never repeated in the same game and must be at least 300 DIP from the pointer.
   - No spot is ever on another monitor or under a full-screen app.
   - The spot is recomputed if the window moves or closes; she then slides to the nearest new edge.
4. **Cues while hidden.**
   - Every 8 ± 2 s: a giggle «هِهِ» in gold near her and a leaf wiggle. A sound plays only if sounds are on (owner decision).
   - After 20 s (the existing `HintSeconds`): a stronger wiggle, and she shows a bit more of herself.
5. **Warmer or colder.**
   - The trigger is the pointer stopping for 400 ms or more, at most once every 3 s.
   - A small whisper chip appears beside the pointer for 1.2 s:
     - closer than 200 DIP: «دافي… دافي كتير!» (warm tint, gold);
     - closer than 450 DIP: «دافي…»;
     - otherwise: «برد…» or «برد… بعيد كتير» (cold tint, snowflake icon).
   - When the pointer is within 120 DIP she trembles from the giggle: a ±1.5 px shake.
   - No hints during the first 5 s.
6. **Found.** A click on any visible part of her makes her pop out with a squash-and-stretch hop.
   - She uses happy eyes, 4 gold sparks burst out, and a bubble shows «لقيتني! بـ ١٢ ثانية».
   - A pip fills, then the next round starts after 2 s.
7. **Give up.** After 60 s (`GiveUpSeconds`), or if he right-clicks or presses Esc once (Esc twice quits):
   - she peeks out with the `tease` pose: one eye winks and her tongue shows;
   - a bubble says «هون كنت!»;
   - a dashed outline marks the spot for 1.5 s.
8. **End.** After 3 rounds an ask bubble shows the result in one line: «لقيتني مرتين من تلاتة. منعيدها؟» with «يلا» / «خلص». A score of 0 reads «ما لقيتني ولا مرة. منعيدها؟».

Rules kept from 0.9: the game catcher, the 3 rounds, `HintSeconds` 20 and `GiveUpSeconds` 60. Under reduced motion she teleports instead of flying, and the chips and hints show without movement.

New core (Sahar.Core.Games): `HideSeek.Spots(windows, taskbar, monitors, pointer, rng)` returns ranked candidates, and `HideSeek.Warmth(distanceDip)` returns `Hot / Warm / Cold / Far`.

## 6. Chat history storage

- **Where.** In the Mind's personal folder, through the existing `SecretBox`: AES-GCM, a DPAPI-wrapped key, and the cross-process mutex.
- **Files:**
  - `chats/index` is encrypted: id, title, created, last, count. Titles are content too.
  - `chats/<id>` is encrypted: messages `{at, who: me|her, kind: text|draft|ask|receipt, text, ask: {question, answer: yes|no|timeout}}`.
  - Writes are atomic through SecretBox's write path. A damaged file is moved aside as `*.bad`, as the box already does.
- **Retention.** It follows `MindEpisodeDays` (7 / 30 / 90; default 30), using the same clamp as `MindStore.Prune`.
  - Messages older than the cut are removed at start and once a day.
  - A conversation with nothing left disappears.
  - The list footer states the live number of days.
- **«انسي كل شي».** `SecretBox.ForgetAll` deletes the key first (crypto-shred), then the folder, so chats go with it.
  - An open chat window is told through the existing erase hook and drops to the empty state at once.
  - Settings «امسح كل شي» (EraseAll / Shred) covers the folder too.
- **Locked or forgotten box** (`MindStoreException` locked/forgotten/busy). Chat still works for the session but is not kept. The list shows one calm line: «ما قدرت احفظ الحكي هلق».
- **Logs.** Never text: only counts, kinds, lengths and ids. This is the same rule as `mind: draft shown (n chars)`.
- **Never** synced, never in notifications, never in crash dumps; the engine log policy is unchanged.
- **Open question.** Should storage follow the «تعلّم مني» consent, or be separate? See §9.

## 7. Native windows to replace, and the technology

| Surface | Tech | Why |
|---|---|---|

## 10. Quran player bar (owner ruling 6) — `V15QuranBar`, board `V15Quran` / `V15QuranDark`

### 10.1 What the code does today (0.14.0)

- **Audio source.** One MP3 per surah: `Quran.Url` → `cdn.islamic.network/quran/audio-surah/128/{reciter}/{surah}.mp3`.
- **Player.** `MediaRecitation` wraps Windows `MediaPlayer` (AutoPlay) and is kept out of the system media controls.
- **What `IRecitation` exposes.** Only `Start(url)`, `Opened`, `Ended`, `Failed` and `Dispose`.
- **State.** `QuranTool.State` is idle / opening / playing / ended / stopped / failed. `Stop()` exists. There is a 15 s open timeout. Settings keep `QuranLastSurah`.

### 10.2 What is feasible

| Control | Feasible now? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Play / pause | Yes | Add `Pause()` / `Resume()` to `IRecitation` → `MediaPlayer.Pause/Play` |

### 10.3 Design

- **Hidden on the tag.** While a recitation plays, the tag reads «الكهف ١٢:٠٤».
- **Opening the bar.** A click on the tag, or Space while the tag has focus, grows it into the bar. The bar hangs from the same thread, is 340 wide, radius 20, uses the menu colour and the card shadow, and enters with the standard pop animation.
- **Row 1.** A tint book badge, the surah name (15/600) and the reciter (12 t2). The state is appended: «· واقفة» or «· عم تفتح…». A close button (×) stops and closes.
- **Row 2.**
  - Elapsed time on the right and total on the left, both tabular.
  - A 4 px sunken track with an accent fill **from the right** and a 14 px knob.
  - The track is a real slider (role slider, aria-valuetext «١٢:٠٤ من ٤٣:١٠»).
- **Row 3, from right to left:** السورة يلي قبل · ارجع ١٠ · **[play/pause, 48 px accent circle]** · قدّم ١٠ · السورة الجاية.
- **Direction (decided).** Time runs right → left, like reading.
  - "Back" icons point right and "forward" icons point left.
  - The play triangle points left.
  - Keys follow the screen: → is back 10 s, ← is forward 10 s, Ctrl+← is the next surah, Ctrl+→ is the previous one, Space is play/pause, Esc collapses.
  - This is a deliberate departure from Material's unmirrored media icons, chosen because the whole app is RTL.
- **Collapsing.** The bar shrinks back to the tag after 6 s with no pointer over it, or on Esc or an outside click.
- **States:**
  - **playing.**
  - **paused.** The big button becomes «كمّل».
  - **opening.** Controls are at 38% opacity and disabled; times show «--:--».
  - **failed.** «ما قدرت شغّل التلاوة هلق. يمكن النت ضعيف.» with «جرّب كمان مرة» / «سكّر».
- **Tech.** Composition + D2D inside the tag visual, so no new window. Only the open bar takes input (a hit-test region); the tag stays click-through as today.
