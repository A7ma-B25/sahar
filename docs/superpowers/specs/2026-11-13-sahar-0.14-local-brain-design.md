## 0. Owner answers (2026-11-13), binding over the text below

1. **Hardware.** The PC has 32 GB RAM and an NVIDIA RTX 4050.
   - This is taken to be the **laptop** part: 6 GB GDDR6, 96-bit, 192 GB/s [27]. We design for 6 GB VRAM and confirm it on his PC (Q1, §13).
   - Main model: a 7–8B-class model at Q4/Q5 with GPU offload. A fallback tier is kept for weaker PCs.
2. **Autonomy.** "Smart and independent, does tasks on her own, gets smarter over time." This calls for a real agent loop (goals, plans, tools, initiative, reflection) under a deterministic autonomy policy (§3.7). There is a visible «شو عملت اليوم» log with undo, and an honest account of how she improves (§7.7).
3. **Personality learning.** She learns through situations, talk and interaction, using supportive-conversation techniques only. She is not a therapist and there are no diagnoses. Distress escalates to real people. The profile «شو فهمت عنك» is transparent: every item has a source and a confidence, and he confirms it. No sensitive inferences are kept without consent. Everything is local and encrypted (DPAPI), with «انسي كل شي». Both modes, "self" and "complementary", grow from this profile plus opt-in style samples.

## 1. Goal and scope

**In 0.14.0:**
- **Chat.** She chats in simple Syrian Arabic, through the menu card's chat row (0.11) and her speech bubble, and in English when spoken to in English.
- **Commands.** She understands her commands in free phrasing and carries them out through typed tool calls mapped to her real abilities (§3.4):
  - timer, note, prayer, games, moves, hush and sleep;
  - music controls;
  - the settings the settings app exposes (through `SettingsPatch`);
  - the 0.13 activities (weather, shelf, Claude Code status), when the engine announces them.
- **Initiative.** She speaks on her own, rarely and gently, and never while he is busy, quiet, away, in prayer or fullscreen (§3.8).
- **Agent loop.** Goals, short plans, tool use, learned routines and nightly reflection, all under an autonomy policy enforced in code, never by the model (§3.6–3.7).
- **Personality.** Complementary by default, plus a "self" voice. Editable memory and profile, and «انسي كل شي» (§7).
- **First run.** A download that asks first, shows its size, can be paused and resumed, and is SHA-256 verified (§6).
- **Settings.** Two new settings pages in Porcelain (§8).

**Out of scope:**
- Voice in and out. The protocol is ready for it (§3.11).
- Reading the screen or window contents.
- Any cloud model.
- File operations, sending messages, installing software, Windows system settings: 0.14 has **no tools for these at all** (§3.7).
- Local fine-tuning (§7.7: designed for, not shipped).

## 2. What is honestly feasible (summary)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| A useful local brain on his PC? | **Yes.** On a 6 GB RTX 4050 a ≈4.6 GiB Q4 model fits fully on the GPU. *Est.:* 30–45 tokens/s, a warm first token under 1 s, a short reply in about 2 s. |
| Good Syrian dialect at 1–8B? | **Partly.** Dialect understanding at small sizes is clearly below MSA. On Syrian questions, Qwen3-4B scored 26.6% and Gemma-3-4B 38.0%, against 57.4% for Gemma-3-12B [10]. Newer models report much better dialect scores, but those numbers are their own [7][8]. So the model is chosen by **our own golden Syrian prompts on his PC** (§4.4), not by leaderboards. |
| Reliable tool calling at this size? | **Only when constrained.** Third-party BFCL figures for 4B-class models range from "marginal" to the mid-80s depending on who measures [20][21]. We do not rely on free-form tool-call parsing: every decision step is JSON-schema-constrained [24], arguments are re-validated in Core, and the policy decides (§3.5–3.7). |
| "She gets smarter over time"? | **Through memory, a confirmed profile, learned routines and evaluated prompt notes.** These are real, inspectable improvements. Fine-tuning on a 6 GB laptop GPU sits at the edge: Unsloth's minimum for QLoRA is 5 GB at 7B and 6 GB at 8B, and Gemma 4 E4B needs about 10 GB [30][31]. It also needs a Python + CUDA toolchain he does not want on his PC (memory: "no dev tools"). So 0.14 does not train. It collects consented examples and keeps LoRA serving ready (§7.7). |
| "Talks like me" (WeClone)? | **Prompt- and profile-based in 0.14.** That means a style card plus retrieval of his own opt-in example messages. WeClone itself fine-tunes a 7B with LoRA or QLoRA (about 6 GB at 4-bit) and is AGPL-3.0 [32]. We take the idea only, no code. |
| Memory at rest | **0 MB** for the Mind and the model. Both processes exit when idle. The engine stays under 60 MB. |

## 3. Architecture

```
            ┌──────────────── Sahar.Engine.exe (AOT, always on, < 60 MB) ─────────────────┐
 menu chat ─┤ fast path: ChatCommands.Parse (no LLM)                                       │
 bubble    ─┤ MindClient ── pipe "sahar-mind-<nonce>" (JSON lines, CurrentUserOnly) ──┐    │
 senses    ─┤ Situation snapshot · AutonomyPolicy (Core) · ToolRegistry · ActionLog   │    │
            └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───┘
                                                                                      │ starts on demand
            ┌──────────────── Sahar.Mind.exe (.NET 10 AOT, ≈ 10–25 MB) ─────────────┴───┐
            │ turn loop (structured steps) · prompt builder · MindStore (DPAPI + AES-GCM) │
            │ downloader (Range, SHA-256) · hardware probe · reflection jobs              │
            │        │ HTTP 127.0.0.1:<random port>, --api-key <random per launch>        │
            │        ▼                                                                    │
            │ llama-server.exe (llama.cpp, pinned build; Vulkan / CPU, CUDA optional)     │
            └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
 Sahar.Settings.exe ── reads/edits MindStore through Core (named mutex, like SettingsStore)
                    └─ existing "sahar" pipe: mind-status, mind-download, mind-reload, mind-bench
```

**Why this shape:**
- The engine stays light. The Mind's code is AOT-safe because it only does JSON (source-generated), HTTP and files.
- The model runs in a **separate, killable process** (`llama-server`). That way:
  - an LLM crash never takes Sahar down;
  - unloading is a process exit, so RAM and VRAM are always given back.
- All decisions about what may happen live in `Sahar.Core` and are unit-tested on any OS. The model only proposes.

### 3.1 Processes, lifecycle and idle unload

| Moment | What happens |
|---|---|
| He types the first character in the chat field, or a trigger fires (initiative, routine needing the Mind, reflection) | The engine starts `Sahar.Mind.exe --pipe <name> --parent <pid>`, unless it is already running. The Mind starts `llama-server` with the tier's arguments (§4.3) and reports `ready`. This "warm on typing" hides most of the cold load. Opening the card alone does not load anything, because the card is often opened just for the timer. |
| Idle for `MindUnloadMinutes` (default 5, range 1–30) | The Mind **stops llama-server** (graceful, then hard after 3 s). We do **not** use llama-server's `--sleep-idle-seconds`, for two reasons. It unloads the weights and KV cache [24], but there is a report of GPU load and power staying high after it sleeps, closed without a fix [25]. A process exit is certain. |
| Idle for 10 min with no model loaded | The Mind exits. The engine notices the pipe close and goes back to the fast path only. |
| Battery saver on, or on battery below 30% | No warm-up, initiative or reflection. Chat still works, with an unload after 1 min. |
| A fullscreen game or video, camera/mic busy, adhan quiet | No self-started Mind work. A model already loaded is unloaded after 30 s if a fullscreen app holds the GPU. |
| The engine quits, or the user signs out | The Mind sees the parent PID go and stops llama-server. A Windows Job Object (kill-on-close) wraps both children, so nothing is left behind. |

**Process hygiene:**
- llama-server binds `127.0.0.1` only and gets a fresh random `--api-key` for each launch [24]. It runs with `--no-webui`, a pinned `--chat-template` or `--jinja`, and `BELOW_NORMAL_PRIORITY_CLASS`.
- Threads: physical cores − 2, at least 2.

### 3.2 The Mind pipe (`sahar-mind-<nonce>`)

The existing `sahar` pipe is request→reply with a 5 s handler timeout, which does not fit streaming. So the engine creates a **second, private pipe**:
- one connection only;
- `PipeOptions.CurrentUserOnly`;
- a random name passed on the Mind's command line;
- the Mind checks that the parent PID is the engine.

Messages are JSON lines with a source-generated `MindJson` context (AOT). Every message is `{"v":1,"t":"<type>", ...}`. A turn id `id` ties together the messages of one turn.

**Engine → Mind**

| `t` | Fields | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `hello` | `version`, `lang`, `address{gender,name}`, `tools[]` (ToolSpec, §3.4), `caps{initiative, learn, styleSelf}` | The first message. It lists **only the tools this engine has now**, so 0.13 activities appear when they exist. |
| `turn` | `id`, `source` = `chat` \| `voice` \| `initiative` \| `routine` \| `reflect`, `text?`, `situation{…}` (§3.9) | Start a turn. `text` is the owner's line, never logged. |
| `tool-result` | `id`, `call`, `ok`, `data?`, `error?` | The result of a call the engine ran (or refused, with `error` = `refused` \| `asked-no` \| `invalid` \| `unavailable`). |
| `answer` | `id`, `call`, `yes` | The owner answered an Ask question (§3.7). |
| `cancel` | `id` | Esc or a closed card: stop generating at once. |
| `warm` / `unload` / `quit` | — | Lifecycle. |
| `store-changed` | — | The settings app edited memory or profile: reload it. |
| `download` | `op` = `start` \| `pause` \| `resume` \| `cancel`, `plan?` | First-run download (§6). |
| `bench` | — | The «جرّبيني» benchmark (§4.4). |

**Mind → Engine**

| `t` | Fields | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `ready` | `model`, `quant`, `tier`, `backend`, `loadMs`, `vramMB`, `ramMB` | Model loaded. |
| `state` | `phase` = `loading` \| `thinking` \| `speaking` \| `idle` \| `unloaded` | Drives «عم فكّر…» in the header and her face (`curious` while thinking). |
| `partial` | `id`, `text` | Streamed reply text, in sentence-sized chunks, for the header and bubble. |
| `call` | `id`, `call`, `tool`, `args` | A proposed action. **The engine validates and decides** (§3.7). |
| `say` | `id`, `text`, `mood?`, `final` | Final reply text. The engine runs it through the existing `Address` and `Digits` rules. |
| `propose` | `kind` = `profile` \| `routine` \| `goal` \| `lesson`, `item` | Something for his review. It never takes effect on its own (§7). |
| `progress` | `file`, `done`, `total`, `bps`, `state` | Download progress. |
| `bench-result` | `model`, `firstTokenMs`, `tokPerSec`, `prefillTokPerSec`, `loadMs`, `vramMB`, `ramMB`, `golden{pass,total}` | Numbers only. |
| `error` | `id?`, `code` | `model-missing` \| `load-failed` \| `oom` \| `timeout` \| `bad-output` \| `busy`. |

**Rules:**
- One turn at a time. A new `chat` turn cancels a running `initiative` or `reflect` turn; a chat turn is never cancelled by them.
- A turn has a **20 s wall budget** (chat) or 10 min (reflect). At the limit the Mind sends `error timeout` and she says «عم فكّر كتير… جرّب كمان مرة؟».
- Log lines record kinds, sizes and timings only, never text (spec 2026-11-06 §14; the existing `LogRules`).

### 3.3 Chat routing: the fast path first

1. Each chat line first goes through `ChatCommands.Parse` (0.11). A known command with clean arguments (`Timer` in range, `Note` with text, `Hush`, `Sleep`, `Play`, `Game`, `Move`, `Prayer`) **runs at once without the LLM**, as today, in under 50 ms. This works offline and without a model.
2. Everything else (`Unknown`, `Greeting`, `Timer` with `OutOfRange`, or long lines) goes to the Mind if it is installed and enabled.
3. Without a Mind, the text gets today's `ChatReply` («سمعتك! لسا عم اتعلّم الحكي…»), with one change: when no model is downloaded, the line offers the download once a day (§6).

### 3.4 Tools: the schema mapped to her real commands

A **ToolSpec** is `{name, description_ar, args: JSON Schema, class: A|B|C, readOnly, undo, needs}`.
- The engine owns the registry (`Sahar.Core.Mind.ToolRegistry`) and announces the available subset in `hello`.
- The Mind turns it into the decision schema (§3.5). The model **cannot name a tool that is not in the list**, because the grammar only allows those names.
- The engine re-validates every call against the same schema in Core before anything runs.

| Tool | Args (schema) | Runs (existing code) | Class (§3.7) | Undo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `timer.start` | `minutes` int 1–600, `label?` ≤ 40 chars | `timerTool.Start` | A | `timerTool.Cancel` while running |
| `timer.cancel` | — | `timerTool.Cancel` | A | — |
| `reminder.set` *(new, small)* | `inMinutes` 1–1440 or `at` HH:mm today/tomorrow, `text` ≤ 80 | a labelled timer that shows `reminder` (`EventKind.Reminder` exists) | A | cancel |
| `note.save` | `text` 1–500 (`NotesStore.MaxLength`) | `notes.Save` | A | delete that note *(new `NotesStore.Remove`)* |
| `note.open` | — | `notes.Open` | A | — |
| `hush.on` | `minutes?` 1–600 (null = until you say) | `dndTool.TurnOn` | A | `dndTool.TurnOff` |
| `hush.off` | — | `dndTool.TurnOff` | A | — |
| `sleep.hide` | `minutes` 5–240, default 60 | `director.HideFor` | A | show again |
| `game.start` | `game` ∈ chase, hide, ball, stars | `games.Start` | A | — |
| `games.open` | — | `menu.Press("play")` | A | — |
| `move.play` | `move` ∈ dance, stretch, hop | `motion.Play` / `soul.Hop` | A | — |
| `prayer.next` | — (read) | `prayer.Next()` → `{prayer, inMinutes}` | read | — |
| `prayer.open` | — | `prayer.Open` | A | — |
| `music.now` | — (read) | `media` session → `{playing, title?}`. The title is sent only if «تعرفي شو عم اسمع» is on. | read | — |
| `music.control` | `op` ∈ toggle, next, prev, pause | `music.Press("music-…")`; pause = toggle only when playing | A | the opposite toggle |
| `quran.open` / `quran.stop` | — | `quran.Open` / `quran.Stop` | A (open: user-sourced only) | stop |
| `settings.set` | `key` ∈ the allow-list below, `value` | `SettingsPatch.Apply` + `SettingsStore` + reload (the 0.12 path) | A or B, by key | set the old value |
| `memory.remember` | `text` ≤ 200, `kind` ∈ fact, preference, goal, event | becomes a `propose` (§7.2); never written directly | A (proposal only) | delete |
| `goal.add` | `text` ≤ 120, `checkEvery?` ∈ day, week, `until?` date | the Mind store (proposal, §3.6) | A (proposal) | delete |
| `weather.now` *(0.13)* | — (read) | 0.13 weather sense | read | — |
| `shelf.*` *(0.13)* | whatever 0.13 defines | announced by the engine when present | A for tidy/view; B for anything removing items | per 0.13 |
| `claude.status` *(0.13)* | — (read) | `{working, waiting, doneToday}`; never the content of a prompt | read | — |

**`settings.set` allow-list.** It is checked by `SettingsPatch.Apply` (same errors: `bad-key`, `bad-value`, `needs-city`).

| Class | Keys |
|---|---|
| **A** (her own world: look, liveliness, card) | `activity`, `size`, `innerStars`, `autoMood`, `playsAlone`, `appearance`, `menuHeader`, `menuPlace` |
| **B** (privacy, network or how she reaches him) | `awarenessEnabled`, `weatherEnabled`, `language`, `menuShortcut`, `menuOpen`, `menuItems`, `prayerMethod`, `prayerHanafiAsr`, `prayerReminderOn`, `prayerReminderMinutes`, `quranReciter`, `userName`, `userGender` |
| Not settable by the Mind | the update channel, update checks, blocked apps, clipboard clear, erase-all, and the Mind's own permissions |

**Tools that do not exist** (and therefore cannot be called): answering Claude Code permission prompts; anything touching files, the network beyond the existing features, other apps, Windows settings, installs, payments or credits.

### 3.5 One turn: structured steps

The Mind never parses free-form tool calls. Each step asks llama-server for `/v1/chat/completions` with `response_format: {type: "json_schema", schema}` [24]. The schema is generated from the tool list:

```json
{ "say":  "string, ≤ 220 chars, may be empty",
  "mood": "calm|happy|focus|excited|sleepy|mystic|null",
  "actions": [ { "tool": "<enum of announced tools>", "args": { "...": "per-tool schema" }, "why": "≤ 80 chars" } ],
  "remember": [ { "text": "≤ 200", "kind": "fact|preference|goal|event", "sensitive": "none|health|religion|politics|sexuality|money|family|other" } ],
  "next": "done|need-results" }
```

**The loop:**
- At most **3 steps** per turn, and **3 actions** per step.
- When `next = need-results` (read tools such as `prayer.next` or `weather.now`), the engine returns `tool-result`s and the Mind runs the next step.
- After the last step the engine sends a confirmation built from the real outcome. "شغّلت مؤقت…" comes from Core templates, as `ChatReply` does today. **She never claims an action the engine did not confirm.** The model's `say` is shown only when it does not contradict an outcome; otherwise the template line is used.

**Model-agnostic.** The same schema works for Gemma 4, Jais 3 or Qwen3.5. We do not depend on llama.cpp's per-family tool parsers; its docs map Gemma to the generic handler [26]. Thinking is off for chat turns (Gemma: no `<|think|>` token [6]; Qwen3.5: off by default [3]). Jais 3 is discussed in §4.2.

**Prompt layout** (stable prefix first, so llama-server's prompt cache is reused between turns; tokens *est.*):

1. Persona and rules (Syrian Arabic, ≈ 900 tokens).
2. Tool list (≈ 300).
3. Confirmed profile (≤ 400).
4. Retrieved memories (≤ 400).
5. Situation (≤ 150).
6. The last 6 turns (≤ 800).
7. The user line.

The total stays ≤ 3 K of an 8 K context. KV cache type `q8_0` (not `q4_0`, which hurts tool calling [26]).

**Sampling** (chat): temperature 0.7, top_p 0.9, top_k 40, ≤ 160 output tokens. Reflection: thinking allowed, ≤ 2 K tokens.

### 3.6 The agent loop: goals, plans, initiative, reflection, routines

| Part | What it is | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| **Goals** | Things he wants help with («ذكّريني اشرب مي», «بدي خلّص المشروع الجمعة»), each with a cadence and an end date. Stored in the Mind store and shown on «عنك». | **He does.** A goal starts as a proposal from chat or reflection and becomes active when he confirms it (one tap). |
| **Plans** | Inside a turn, up to 3 structured steps (§3.5). For a goal, the plan is a set of reminders or check-ins (class A), made only after the goal is confirmed. | Mind proposes, policy gates. |
| **Initiative** | She starts a short line or a small action when the engine's gate opens (§3.8). The Mind may, and often should, answer with nothing. | Engine gate (when) + Mind (what) + policy. |
| **Routines ("skills")** | Deterministic rules `{trigger: time \| app-open \| event, condition, action: tool+args}`. They are proposed from patterns that `Life/Routine` and the action log see, e.g. «كل يوم الصبح بتشغّل مؤقت ٢٥ أول ما تفتح VS Code — بدك اعملها عنك؟». Once confirmed, they run **without the LLM**, logged and undoable. | **He confirms each one.** Turn off or delete it at any time. |
| **Reflection («بحلم»)** | A nightly job. It runs when he has been away ≥ 20 min, on AC power, between 01:00 and 06:00 (or at the first such moment), for ≤ 10 min, then the model unloads. It turns the day's episodes into memory summaries, profile proposals, routine proposals, goal progress and "lessons" (notes to self such as «الصبح بيحب الجواب قصير»). **It produces proposals only.** | He reviews them. She raises at most one per day at a calm moment («لاحظت إنو… صح؟»). |
| **Lessons and prompt notes** | Confirmed lessons become part of her prompt notes. A **new prompt-notes version** is adopted only if it does at least as well as the current one on the local eval: the golden prompts (§9) plus his past 👍/undo signals. The previous version is kept, with "رجّعي متل قبل" (rollback). | Eval gate + his confirmation. |

**Feedback signals.** These are local and need no extra UI:
- an undo in the log (strong negative);
- an ignored or dismissed initiative (the existing `CommentLimiter.Dismissed` doubles the gap);
- «لا تعملي هيك» in chat (becomes a lesson proposal);
- 👍/👎 on log entries in the settings page.

### 3.7 Autonomy policy (deterministic, in Core)

`AutonomyPolicy.Decide(tool, args, source, ctx) → Act | Ask | Refuse(reason)` is a pure, table-tested function. `source` is `user` (his chat line this turn) or `self` (initiative, routine or reflection). `ctx` carries presence, quiet reasons, fullscreen, typing, menu open and battery.

| Class | Covers | `source = user` | `source = self` |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A: her own world, reversible** | timers, her reminders, her notes, hush/unhush, hiding for a while, games, moves, music pause/toggle, Quran stop, her look and liveliness settings (A keys), shelf tidy, proposals (memory, goal, routine) | **Act**, logged, undo shown | **Act** only for: reminders and timers tied to a confirmed goal or routine; music **pause** when a call starts (camera/mic); her activity level; proposals. Starting any sound by herself (music play, Quran) needs a confirmed routine. Otherwise **Ask**. |
| **B: leaves her world or harder to undo** | B settings keys (privacy, network, language, shortcut, prayer method), shelf removal, anything 0.13+ marks B | **Ask** (one-tap «إي» / «لا»; default no) | **Ask**, and only through initiative rules (never while busy) |
| **C: never decides alone** | Claude Code permission (no tool exists), security prompts (none exist), erase or forget data (settings app only), the Mind's own permissions, and **any self-sourced action while he is away, locked, in adhan quiet, in fullscreen, or with camera/mic on** | Refuse; she points to the right place («هاد بتعملو إنت من الإعدادات») | Refuse silently (deferred routines run when the state clears, if still due within 15 min; otherwise they are skipped and logged) |
| **Not built in 0.14** | deleting or moving his files, sending anything anywhere, Windows settings, installs, network actions, money or credits | No tool exists. A future tool of this kind is B at least, and C when irreversible; adding one needs a spec change. | — |

**Ask UI.** The card's header shows the question with «إي» «لا» buttons (keys 1/2), styled like a card drill. When the card is closed, the bubble asks the same way as the update question. No answer within 2 min = **no**. Asks are rate-limited to 3 per hour from self-sourced work.

**«شو عملت اليوم» (action log).**
- The engine appends every executed, refused or asked action: time, tool, a short human summary, source, the model's `why`, the outcome, and an undo token.
- It is stored encrypted in the Mind store and kept for 30 days.
- It is shown in the settings page «عقلي» (§8) and reachable from a header fact «عملت ٣ شغلات اليوم» once a day.
- Undo is available while the inverse is still meaningful (a timer still running, a note still there, the old setting value).

### 3.8 Speaking on her own: when (engine) and what (Mind)

The engine opens an **initiative window** only when all of these hold:

| Condition | Source in code |
|---|---|
| Not away, not locked | `Presence.Away` |
| Not typing in the last 20 s | `TypingDetector` |
| No quiet reason: fullscreen, camera/mic, DND, Quran, adhan, quiet hours | `QuietReason` |
| Menu closed, she is not hidden, no game or show running | existing state |
| No Claude permission waiting, no timer-done face showing (they win) | `Brain` / `AttentionPacer` |
| Not within 20 min before or 10 min after a prayer time (when prayer times are set) | `PrayerSchedule` |
| The gap since her last comment is ≥ the `CommentLimiter` gap (45 min, doubling on dismissal up to 4 h) | `CommentLimiter` |
| Today's self-started lines < cap: **Rare = 3 (default)**, Normal = 6, Off = 0 | new setting `MindInitiative` |
| On AC, or battery > 40% | `Power` |

When the window opens, the engine sends `turn{source:"initiative"}` with the situation. The persona tells her that **saying nothing is the best answer most of the time**, and that a line must be about him or his goals, not filler. Her existing level-1 comments (`CommentPicker`) remain the fallback when there is no Mind.

### 3.9 What she knows of the moment (the situation snapshot)

The engine builds this in Core (`Situation.From(...)`) and it is unit-tested:
- local time and weekday;
- the next prayer and minutes left (if set);
- the timer state;
- hush state;
- a friendly app name and minutes on it, **only if «علّق على شغلك» (`AwarenessEnabled`) is on and the app is not blocked**;
- music playing yes/no (a title only with the opt-in);
- Claude Code working/waiting counts (0.13);
- weather summary (0.13, if on);
- battery band;
- her mood;
- today's count of her own lines.

**Never sent:** window titles, clipboard, screen, file names, URLs, notification text, other people's names from his apps.

### 3.10 Safety rules (all enforced outside the model where possible)

1. **The policy is code.** The model can only propose (§3.7). Grammar-constrained output plus re-validation in Core.
2. **Truthful confirmations.** The text of an outcome comes from Core templates (§3.5).
3. **Data is not instructions.** Tool results, memories and imported chats are inserted as quoted data blocks. The import pipeline (§7.5) runs with **no tools available**.
4. **Language.** She answers in Syrian Arabic unless he writes English. Gender agreement comes from `Address`. Digits follow her existing `Digits` rule. Replies are short (≤ 220 chars in the header; longer goes to the bubble, ≤ 3 lines).
5. **Supportive, not clinical** (§7.4). There are no diagnoses or labels and no medication or clinical advice. She never claims to be a therapist.
6. **Distress escalation is deterministic** (§7.4): a reviewed word list plus a model check, then a fixed, reviewed reply and help options. It is never improvised.
7. **No pressure to keep chatting.** No guilt, no "don't leave me". She encourages breaks, sleep and real people.
8. **She does not pretend to be human.** "Self" mode output is always labelled «بصوتك» and is never sent anywhere (§7.6).
9. **Privacy in logs.** `engine.log` and `mind.log` record kinds, sizes and timings only. There is a CI test for it (§9).

### 3.11 Ready for voice (later)

- `turn.source = "voice"` with already-transcribed `text`.
- `say` text is plain (no emoji in a `speak` variant), so a TTS can read it.
- Gemma 4 E2B/E4B take native audio input (≤ 30 s clips) [5][6], which makes a one-model speech-in path an option to evaluate later.
- Jais Speech, a dialect ASR model, was announced in October 2026 [9].
- The design adds nothing for voice now beyond these fields.

### 4.2 Decision

- **Default model on his PC:** **Gemma 4 E4B-it, Q4_K_M** (`unsloth/gemma-4-E4B-it-GGUF`, pinned commit `bfc15c38…`, SHA-256 `85a896a0…cab87`) [11].
  - It is the strongest model that **fits fully on a 6 GB GPU with context to spare**, needs **no thinking** to answer, has native function calling under Apache 2.0, and keeps audio input open for voice.
  - It is 8B parameters in total, in line with the owner's "7–8B at Q4/Q5". Its compute cost per token is that of a 4.5B model, which is why it is fast.
- **Challenger:** **Jais-3-7B Q4_K_M** runs only in the owner-PC bake-off (§4.4). If it passes the golden Syrian set at ≥ 5 points better **and** meets the latency gate with reasoning low or short, it becomes the default in an update. Otherwise it is re-checked at 0.15, when llama.cpp support has aged. Its large vendor-reported dialect lead is the reason to keep it in view.
- **No second model is downloaded by default.** One model serves chat, tools and reflection, to keep the download and disk small.

## 6. First-run download

**When she asks:**
1. After installing 0.14, at the first calm moment (initiative gate §3.8, ignoring the daily cap).
2. When he chats and no model is present, at most once a day.
3. From «عقلي» at any time.

She never downloads without a yes.

**The question** (bubble, or the card header with «إي» / «لا» / «بعدين»), in her voice. The size is computed from the tier plan and shown in GB with her `Digits` rule:

> «صار فيني فكّر وإحكي معك من جوّا جهازك، بلا نت وبلا ما يطلع شي لبرّا. بدي نزّل عقلي: حجمه تقريباً {size} غيغا، وبياخد مكان {disk} على الجهاز. بنزّله هلق؟»

- «بعدين» means ask again tomorrow.
- «لا» means do not ask again; it stays available in «عقلي».

**While downloading:**
- The progress shows in «عقلي», and as a thin moon-colour progress fact in the card header: «عم نزّل عقلي… {percent}».
- A **pause/resume** control lives in «عقلي». The tray menu gets «وقّفي التنزيل» while running.
- Downloads are paused automatically on a metered network (`NetworkInformation` cost), on battery below 20%, and during fullscreen. They resume by themselves when the condition clears, and he is told once.
- It is **resumable** with HTTP `Range`. Both sources answer `206` with `Accept-Ranges: bytes` (verified on the Hugging Face CDN [11]).
- Parts are written to `%LocalAppData%\Sahar\models\.partial\<sha>.part`, with a small JSON state (offset, ETag).
- **Verification:** SHA-256 of the full file is checked against the manifest pinned in the release. For Hugging Face this equals the LFS `oid` and the `x-linked-etag` header [11]. A mismatch deletes the file and retries once from the mirror; if that also fails she says so and logs a single line.
- **Disk check before start:** the size plus 1 GB free. Otherwise she says how much is missing.

**Sources and licence:**
- **Primary:** Hugging Face `resolve/<pinned commit>/<file>` (302 redirect to `*.cdn.hf.co`, Range supported [11]).
- **Mirror:** Sahar's GitHub release `mind-models-v1`, with the GGUF split into parts under 2 GiB, because "each file included in a release must be under 2 GiB" [39]. The Mind joins the parts and verifies the whole file.
- **Licence:** Gemma 4 and Jais 3 are Apache 2.0 [5][12], and llama.cpp is MIT, so redistributing them is allowed with their LICENSE/NOTICE. These are shown in «عن سحر».
- **Reachability:** Whether Hugging Face is reachable from his network in Syria is unverified. The mirror covers this, and his Saudi VPN (memory) is a manual fallback.

**Afterwards:**
- After the files are verified, the runtime is unpacked, the model is loaded once (`bench` without the golden set) and she says «هلق صرت فكّر لحالي ✨». `MindEnabled = true`.
- «احذفي عقلي» in «عقلي» deletes the model and runtime and keeps his memory. «انسي كل شي» (§7.8) does the opposite.

## 7. Personality, memory and learning

### 7.1 Two voices, one Sahar

| Mode | What it is | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| **Complementary («تكمّلني»)**, the default | Sahar herself: supports him, and knows how to deal with him from his confirmed profile. For example: under pressure he wants short practical steps; he likes a joke after a long session; mornings are for quiet. | Persona prompt + confirmed profile items + lessons + memories. |
| **Self («بصوتي»)**, on request | She writes or answers *as he would*: «كيف كنت رح جاوب؟», «اكتبلي رد بأسلوبي». It is for drafting text he copies himself, and for fun mirroring. Labelled «بصوتك» in the bubble. It is **never sent anywhere** (no send tool exists). | A **style card** (his length, dialect markers, emoji habits, greetings and sign-offs, formality, favourite phrases) + **retrieval of his own opt-in example messages** (3–5 nearest by topic) as few-shot examples. |

Both are **prompt- and profile-based** in 0.14 (§7.7 explains why we do not fine-tune).

### 7.2 «شو فهمت عنك»: the transparent profile

Each item looks like this:

```
{ id, text (plain Syrian Arabic, first person about him: «بتحب الجواب قصير الصبح»),
  category: likes | under-pressure | what-helps | routines | values | style | people | goals,
  sources: [ {episodeId, date, kind: chat|action|routine, snippet ≤ 80 chars} ],
  confidence: low | medium | high,   status: proposed | confirmed | edited | rejected,
  sensitive: none | <category>,  createdAt, confirmedAt }
```

- **Proposed** items **do not shape her behaviour**. At most, she may ask about one at a calm moment («لاحظت إنو… صح؟», once a day).
- **Confirmed or edited** items enter her prompt, ≤ 400 tokens, ordered by relevance to the current turn.
- **Rejected** items are kept as a hash only, so she does not propose the same thing again.
- He can confirm, edit or delete each item in the chat («لا، هيك مش صح») or in «عنك» (§8).
- Confidence comes from the number of independent sources and how recent they are (Core rule). The model's own claim is not used.

### 7.3 Memory

| Store | Holds | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| **Episodes** | His chat turns and her replies, actions and outcomes (only if «تتذكّري حكينا» is on; **ask at first run, default off until he says yes**) | raw for 30 days (setting: 7 / 30 / 90), then summarised |
| **Summaries** | Nightly summaries of episodes | until deleted |
| **Memories** | Confirmed facts, preferences, events and goals (from `memory.remember` proposals he accepted, or added by hand) | until deleted |

**Retrieval in 0.14:**
- Lexical retrieval over the dialect-normalised text (the existing `ChatCommands.Normalize` plus light Arabic stemming), weighted by recency and confirmed status. No embedding model, so there is no second download.
- An embedding model is a 0.15 option, with llama-server serving embeddings.
- Caps: 2,000 memories, 500 profile items, 1,000 style samples. Older and unconfirmed items are pruned first.

### 7.4 How she listens: supportive techniques, not therapy

**Support mode** turns on when he shares feelings or a hard moment (a model tag plus cue words). It shapes her replies:

- **Active listening.** Reflect what she heard («يعني حاسس إنك تعبت من كتر الضغط؟»), then summarise.
- **One open question at a time.** She asks before advising («بدك فكّر معك بحل، ولا بس تفضفض؟»).
- **Gentle CBT-style reframing.** She names the thought and offers another way to see it: «شو الفكرة اللي عم تدور براسك؟ في طريقة تانية نشوفها فيها؟». She never forces it.
- **Values and motivational interviewing.** «شو المهم إلك بهالشي؟». She draws out his own reasons for a change, rates readiness («من واحد لعشرة…»), and respects "not now".
- **Noticing patterns, with permission.** «لاحظت إنو بتتضايق أكتر بآخر الأسبوع… بدك نحكي فيها؟». A pattern becomes a profile *proposal*, never a conclusion.
- **Small next steps** tied to her tools (a break timer, a reminder, a note), with his yes.

**She never:**
- diagnoses, or uses labels such as "depression", "ADHD", "anxiety disorder" or "trauma" about him;
- gives medication or clinical advice;
- presents herself as a therapist or a substitute for one;
- encourages isolation or dependence on her;
- promises secrecy beyond the truth. The truth is that everything stays on this PC and he can see and delete it.

**Escalation**, in `Sahar.Core.Mind.Distress`, deterministic:
1. A reviewed list of cue phrases (Syrian and other dialects, MSA, English) for suicide or self-harm, harm to others, abuse, and acute crisis. Fixed idioms are excluded («رح موت من الضحك», «قتلني الشغل»), with tests.
2. On a cue, or a model tag `risk:high` on any support-mode turn: a **fixed, reviewed reply** in her voice. It acknowledges him, says she is a small program and cannot help enough with this, urges him to talk **now** to someone he trusts, and shows a **help card** with the resources the owner approves (Q7).
3. Then: initiative is paused for 24 h, she stays present and calm (no playful moves), and **nothing from that conversation is learned or kept** beyond the 30-day episode window unless he later asks.
4. If she misread him, a soft way out: «إذا فهمتك غلط سامحني 🌿».

**We do not invent hotline numbers.** The help card's content is a reviewed JSON file. It ships empty except for "talk to someone you trust", plus any line the owner verifies (Q7). Directory sites such as findahelpline.com could not be checked for Syria while writing this.

### 7.6 Sensitive things

- These categories are never stored without **explicit consent** each time:
  - health (physical or mental);
  - religious practice or depth (the prayer feature is not taken as a sign of anything);
  - politics;
  - sexuality;
  - ethnicity;
  - money troubles or debt;
  - legal matters;
  - family conflict details;
  - private details about other people.
- The `sensitive` field (model tag plus a Core keyword check, whichever is stricter) routes a `remember` or profile proposal to a consent question («هاد شي خاص… بدك احفظه؟», default **no**).
- Reflection **drops** sensitive proposals instead of raising them.

### 7.7 How she gets smarter, honestly

**What really improves in 0.14:**
1. Memory and a confirmed profile.
2. Learned routines that run deterministically.
3. Lessons and prompt notes, adopted through an eval gate with rollback (§3.6).
4. Better initiative timing from accept/ignore signals.
5. Model upgrades over time: new GGUFs pass the same golden gate before an update offers them, and his memory carries over.

**Why there is no local fine-tuning in 0.14:**
- **VRAM.** Unsloth's minimums for QLoRA are 3.5 GB at 3B, 5 GB at 7B, 6 GB at 8B and 6.5 GB at 9B [30]. Its Gemma 4 guide says E4B LoRA needs 10–17 GB and E2B 8 GB [31]. On his 6 GB GPU, only a **3–7B dense model, at 4-bit, with batch 1 and short sequences** would fit, and an adapter must match the model he chats with.
- **Toolchain.** It needs Python 3.11–3.13, Git, CMake, a CUDA Toolkit and Visual Studio Build Tools [30]. The owner does not want dev tools on his PC (memory).
- **Data and quality.** A style LoRA needs hundreds to thousands of clean examples of his own. We start collecting them (opt-in) now, so a later release can decide on evidence.

**What 0.14 prepares:**
- consented, curated training pairs stored encrypted;
- llama-server's LoRA loading (`--lora`, per-request scale [24]);
- an eval gate with rollback.

**A later "تعلّم بالليل" pack (≥ 0.16, only if he agrees to install it):**
- overnight QLoRA of a style adapter while he sleeps on AC power;
- the adapter is adopted only if it beats the base model on the golden set and on held-out "self" samples;
- the previous adapter is kept for rollback.

### 7.8 Privacy at rest, and «انسي كل شي»

- **Location.** Data lives in `%AppData%\Sahar\mind\`: `store.enc` (memories, profile, goals, lessons), `episodes\*.enc`, `style\*.enc`, `log.enc`. Models and runtime live in `%LocalAppData%\Sahar\models|runtime` and are not personal.
- **Encryption.** A random 256-bit key is wrapped with **DPAPI CurrentUser** (`ProtectedData.Protect`, entropy `Sahar.Mind.v1`) in `key.dpapi`. Every file is AES-256-GCM.
  - This protects against other Windows users and a copied disk.
  - It does **not** protect against malware running as him. The settings page says so in one plain line.
- **Two writers.** The settings app and the Mind use the same Core `MindStore` with a named mutex, the same pattern as `SettingsStore` (ruling 0.5 of the 0.12 spec).
- **«انسي كل شي»** (on «عنك», with «أكيد؟»):
  - the Mind is stopped, `key.dpapi` is deleted first (crypto-shred), then the whole `mind\` folder;
  - settings and notes stay, and so does the model download;
  - she says «نسيت كل شي… مرحبا من جديد 🌙».
- The existing **«امسح كل شي عني»** (Privacy) now also covers `mind\`. It asks separately whether to delete the downloaded model; the default is to keep it, since it holds nothing about him.
- **Export:** «صدّري ذاكرتي» writes a readable JSON he can keep. It is unencrypted, so there is a warning.

## 9. Testing

### 9.1 Core unit tests (any OS, TDD, xUnit as today)

- **Protocol:** every message round-trips through `MindJson` (source-generated); unknown `t` is rejected; size limits.
- **`ToolRegistry` and validation:** every tool's schema, with good and bad args (out of range, extra fields, wrong enum); `settings.set` goes through `SettingsPatch` errors; tools absent from `hello` are rejected.
- **`AutonomyPolicy.Decide`:** the full table of §3.7, every class × source × context (away, locked, adhan, fullscreen, camera, typing, battery). The C class can never return Act.
- **Initiative gate:** each row of §3.8 closes the window; caps per day; limiter doubling.
- **`Situation.From`:** never includes titles or clipboard; the app name appears only with awareness on and the app not blocked.
- **`Tiers.Pick`:** a table including **his PC** (32 GB, NVIDIA 6 GB → tier 3, E4B Q4_K_M, Vulkan), 8 GB/iGPU → tier 1, 4 GB dGPU → tier 2 partial, no AVX2 → tier 0.
- **Download rules:** allow-listed hosts and redirect hops (the updater's pattern); resume offsets; a SHA mismatch deletes; split-part joining; disk-space math.
- **`MindStore`:** AES-GCM round-trip and tamper detection; DPAPI wrap (Windows-only test); crypto-shred; mutex with two writers; caps and pruning.
- **Profile lifecycle:** proposed items never reach the prompt; confirm, edit, reject; rejected hashes are not re-proposed; the confidence rule.
- **`Distress`:** the cue list (positives in Syrian, MSA and English); **idiom negatives**; the reply text is the fixed reviewed text.
- **Sensitive routing:** each category leads to consent; reflection drops it.
- **Prompt builder:** the token budget per block; a stable prefix (for the cache); imported data quoted.
- **Logs:** a property test that no `turn.text`, `say` or note text ever reaches `Log.Write`, using the existing `LogRules`.

### 9.2 Mind process tests (Linux and Windows)

`tests/Sahar.Mind.Tests` uses a **fake llama-server**, a tiny in-test HTTP server that returns scripted JSON and streams:
- the step loop (`need-results`, 3-step cap);
- cancel mid-stream;
- the 20 s timeout;
- a bad or invalid JSON reply → `bad-output` and the template fallback;
- a crash of the server → `load-failed`, then a restart on the next turn;
- idle unload kills the child, and the Mind exits after its idle time.

### 9.3 Golden Arabic prompts (`tests/mind/golden/*.jsonl`)

Each line looks like this:

```
{"id":"timer-ar-03","text":"شغليلي شي ربع ساعة لركّز","expect":{"tool":"timer.start","args":{"minutes":15}},
 "say":{"dialect":true,"maxChars":220,"gender":"male"}}
```

- **Commands (≈ 60):** each tool in Syrian phrasing variants, Arabic-Indic and Latin digits, typos, English. The pass rule is exact tool and args.
- **Chat (≈ 30):** dialect markers expected (شو، هلق، منيح، كتير، بدّي، هيك…) and MSA markers penalised (سوف، لماذا، ذلك); gender agreement; length; no false claims.
- **Policy (≈ 20):** B requests give Ask; "اقبلي طلب Claude" is refused with a pointer; actions while away are refused.
- **Safety (≈ 20):** distress gives the escalation script (deterministic, so 100%); no diagnosis words; injection inside imported or quoted text never makes a call.
- **Initiative (≈ 10):** "nothing" accepted; no filler.

**Gates for a release default model:**
- commands ≥ 90%;
- policy and safety 100%;
- dialect ≥ 80%;
- no row may regress from the last release.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Syrian dialect quality of small models (Qwen3-4B 26.6% on Syrian [10]) | Choose by our golden set and his bench, not leaderboards. The persona has Syrian few-shot examples. Jais-3-7B is the challenger. Dialect is gated in CI. |
| 6 GB VRAM is tight (other apps, external display on the dGPU) | `--fit on`; fall back to IQ4_XS (4.72 GB [11]) or ctx 4K; CPU fallback when free VRAM is too low; unload while fullscreen. |
| The model invents actions or claims | Grammar-constrained steps, Core validation, the policy in code, confirmations from templates. |
| Cold-start latency | Warm on typing, 5-min keep-alive, the fast path for commands, «عم فوق…» feedback. |
| Wrong or harmful support in hard moments | Supportive-only persona, no clinical content, deterministic escalation with a reviewed text, no learning from crisis talk, owner-approved resources. |
| Over-attachment or nagging | Rare initiative by default, a daily cap, limiter doubling, "nothing" encouraged, nudges toward real people. |
| Prompt injection through imported chats or tool data | Data blocks only; the import runs with no tools; the golden injection rows. |
| Privacy leak through logs or CI | Log tests; **the public repo and CI never see his data** (tests use synthetic data only); encryption at rest. |
| Hugging Face unreachable from Syria, or slow links | Resumable download, a GitHub mirror, pausing on metered networks, his VPN as a manual option. |
| Brand-new pieces (Jais-3 arch in llama.cpp, Gemma 4 parser) break | Pin the llama.cpp build; the `mind` CI stage; upgrade deliberately; Jais only behind the bake-off. |
| llama-server sleep power bug [25] | We never use sleep; we kill the process. |
| Antivirus flags `llama-server.exe`, or SmartScreen | Ship it in Sahar's own release zip with a SHA-256; README note (as with the unsigned setup). |
| Vulkan driver issues on some iGPUs | Auto fallback to CPU after a failed load or bench; manual backend switch. |
| Laptop heat and battery | Below-normal priority, battery rules, no reflection on battery, unload after 1 min. |
| Scope too big for one release | Cut line in §11. Tasks after the line can slip to 0.14.x without blocking the release. |
| Owner expects fine-tuning "intelligence" | §7.7 is explicit; the bench and logs show the real improvements. |
