# Sahar 0.8 — Shake Reactions and Living Moves Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Sahar reacts when you shake her (dizzy, sick, or angry and sulking) and lives on the screen with ground and play moves instead of mostly flying.

**Architecture:** Pure decisions in Sahar.Core (shake detection, which reaction, which living move fits); drawing and motion in Sahar.Engine's MotionController (compositor animations + Ticker steps, like motions 1–25). New faces are expression presets; new colours are two moods. LifeDirector weighs the new moves above flights.

**Tech Stack:** C# .NET 10 Native AOT, Windows.UI.Composition, Direct2D; xUnit; PowerShell UI tests on GitHub Windows runners.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-sahar-windows-v1-design.md (§5 expressions, §6 motions, §8 life) plus the user's 0.8 choices (2026-11-07): shake → dizzy / vomit / angry; walk on the taskbar and sit; stretch and yawn (and scratch her head with her leaf); sit by your window and watch, pretend to type along when you type; play alone (hop between windows, toss her moon, chase a butterfly).

## Global Constraints

- Arabic-first copy, Syrian dialect; her words to you go through `Address.ToYou` (gender).
- No sounds yet (0.8 is silent; voice lines come with the sound plan).
- Privacy §14: no window titles or typed text in engine.log.
- Never steal focus; never block clicks except on her own body.
- Version 0.8.0.

## Review Focus

1. Shaking near a screen edge or across monitors: she never ends off-screen; the reaction plays where she is released.
2. A slow drag back and forth (reading, placing her) is not a shake: needs ≥ 4 direction reversals within 2 s, each swing ≥ 40 DIP.
3. Sulking never traps her: the sulk ends after 20 s or three gentle pokes, also when a needs-you face (timer done, reminder) arrives.
4. Living moves on odd desktops: taskbar on the left/top/auto-hidden, no windows open, a maximized window — she falls back to hover, never walks in mid-air.
5. Typing along never reads keys: only "typing / not typing" (spec §14).

### Task 1: Core — shake detector, reaction choice, faces, moods, motion ids

**Files:** Create `src/Sahar.Core/Motion/Shake.cs`; modify `src/Sahar.Core/Motion/MotionKind.cs`, `src/Sahar.Core/Art/Palette.cs`, `src/Sahar.Core/assets/palette.json`, `src/Sahar.Core/assets/expressions.json`, `src/Sahar.Core/Life/Showcase.cs`; tests in `tests/Sahar.Core.Tests/Motion/ShakeTests.cs` and the catalog/palette tests.

**Produces:**
- `ShakeDetector { Add(double seconds, float xDip); bool Shaken; int Strength; void Reset() }` — a reversal counts when the horizontal direction flips after a swing of ≥ 40 DIP; `Shaken` when ≥ 4 reversals fall within 2 s; `Strength` = the most reversals seen in any 2 s window.
- `enum ShakeReaction { None, Dizzy, Sick, Angry }`; `ShakeMood.Pick(int strength, int recentShakes, double roll)`: strength < 4 → None; recentShakes ≥ 1 (another shake in the last 5 min) → Angry when roll < 0.7 else Dizzy; otherwise strength ≥ 8 → Sick when roll < 0.6 else Dizzy; otherwise Angry when roll < 0.15 else Dizzy.
- Moods `Sick` (green: mid #EAF7E4, bottom #A8D99A, glow #6CC25A) and `Angry` (red: mid #FFE3E0, bottom #F59A92, glow #E0322B).
- Expressions 27 `dizzy` (Calm, Eyes.Dizzy, Mouth.Wobble, Stars), 28 `sick` (Sick, Eyes.Dizzy, Mouth.Puff, Sweat), 29 `angry` (Angry, Eyes.Half, Mouth.Puff, Steam).
- MotionKind 26 Dizzy, 27 Angry, 28 WalkTaskbar, 29 Stretch, 30 Yawn, 31 ScratchHead, 32 WatchYou, 33 TypeAlong, 34 HopWindows, 35 MoonToss, 36 ChaseButterfly; Showcase names for 26–36 and expressions 27–29.

**Tests:** `Shake_SlowDragBackAndForth_IsNot`, `Shake_FourQuickSwings_Is`, `Shake_SmallJitter_IsNot`, `Shake_Strength_CountsTheBestWindow`, `ShakeMood_Pick_*` (each branch at its roll edges), catalog has 30 presets with the three new ones, palette has 16 moods.

### Task 2: Engine — dizzy, sick and angry reactions; sulking

**Files:** `src/Sahar.Engine/Motion/MotionController.cs`, `src/Sahar.Engine/Motion/Reactions.cs` (new partial or helper), `src/Sahar.Engine/App.cs`, `tests/ui/run.ps1`.

- During a drag the detector is fed her centre X in DIPs; on release a shake replaces the throw (no throw), logs `shake: <reaction> (strength N)` and plays the reaction.
- Dizzy (motion 26): face `dizzy` 3.5 s, decaying wobble ±12°, a little sideways sway, then Hover.
- Sick: dizzy wobble 1.2 s, face `sick`, she leans forward, a green drop falls from her mouth 90 DIP and splashes and fades (0.9 s), then a shy face (Eyes.Happy, Mouth.Wobble, blush) 1.5 s, then Hover.
- Angry (motion 27): face `angry`, a puff (two squash pulses), she turns a little away (tilt) and moves 150 DIP away from the pointer; then sulks for 20 s: the face stays angry, the pointer coming near makes her drift away, a click says "هِم! 😤" instead of a poke reaction; the third gentle poke ends the sulk early with "خلص سامحتك 💜" and a happy face; a needs-you face (timer done, reminder, Claude permission) ends it too.
- Test-mode pipe `shake` (args `swings`, `angryNext`) drives a drag with swings and a forced reaction; `debug-shake` reports `last=<reaction> sulking=<bool> strength=N`.

**UI steps (stage `motion`):** shaking 6 swings gives dizzy then Hover; forced Sick shows the sick face and the drop; forced Angry: sulking=true, a click says "هِم! 😤", three pokes end it with "خلص سامحتك 💜"; a slow drag is not a shake.

### Task 3: Engine — living moves 28–36 and life weights

- 28 WalkTaskbar: she lands on the taskbar's edge (the work area's bottom/top/side), walks 200–500 DIP with a step bob (squash 0.96/1.04 per step, 0.35 s), sits (Grounded face extras off) 3 s, then hovers. No taskbar edge (auto-hide or the work area equals the monitor): walk along the monitor's bottom edge.
- 29 Stretch: a cat stretch — scale X 1.18 / Y 0.84 held 0.8 s with Eyes.Closed, Mouth.Flat, then back with a little shake.
- 30 Yawn: Mouth.Yawn, Eyes.Half 1.6 s, slight upward stretch.
- 31 ScratchHead: the leaf pivots ±15° three times fast, Eyes.Look up, Mouth.Flat.
- 32 WatchYou: she moves beside the foreground window's top corner (never over it), looks toward its centre for 6 s.
- 33 TypeAlong: while you type (only the "typing" signal), she sits near the bottom of the foreground window with quick small bobs (0.12 s) and Eyes.Focus for as long as typing lasts (max 20 s).
- 34 HopWindows: up to 3 hops between the tops of visible windows (arcs, landing squash).
- 35 MoonToss: her moon flies up 70 DIP and falls back; she bobs to catch it; twice.
- 36 ChaseButterfly: a small butterfly sprite flutters a wavy path for 5 s and she follows it, then it flies off-screen.
- LifeChooser weights: living moves 28–36 together get at least as much weight as the 10 flights together; TypeAlong only while typing; WatchYou needs a foreground window that is not hers.

**UI steps (stage `life`):** each new move can be forced with `life now=<Kind>` and ends in Hover within its time; TypeAlong with the typing signal on.
